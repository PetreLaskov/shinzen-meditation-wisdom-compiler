# Public Atlas Compiler-To-Atlas Gap Audit - 2026-06-05

This is an internal review note, not evidence for Shinzen claims. It records
the current gap between the internal compiler and the public atlas after the
Windows and Walls, Practice Report Check, Practice Planning Loop, Retreat and
Aftercare, Behavior Change Escalation, Source/Claim Trust, and Asking for
Guidance passes.

## Problem Representation

The compiler is the expert back office: it preserves source posture,
contradictions, safety gates, routing logic, and high-resolution practice
distinctions. The atlas should be the practitioner-facing decision interface:
it should help a serious reader know what to notice, choose, avoid, verify,
and ask for next without exposing internal machinery or sounding like a
machine.

The remaining gap is no longer basic content coverage. The atlas now covers
the main system, methods, transformation mechanisms, safety surfaces, service
aim, Source boundaries, teacher/accountability boundaries, applied-life
domains, and several advanced-reader challenge routes.

The remaining gap is translation of compiler judgment into repeat-use human
practice decisions.

## Added This Session

- `public-atlas/windows-and-walls.md` - makes Shinzen's Wall/Window and
  algorithmic-guidance frame public-useful without romanticizing catastrophe.
  It carries the objective situation, objective behavior, sensory challenge,
  support, safety, and one-method/workout/branching distinctions.
- `public-atlas/practice-report-check.md` - translates the compiler's
  branch-and-loop guidance surface into a self-report tool: context, sensory
  facts, CCE quality, reaction, life effect, and boundary, followed by
  stay/tune/switch/turn-away-or-support/stop-optimizing checks.
- `public-atlas/practice-planning-loop.md` - turns the atlas into a
  repeatable week/month use loop: choose aim, choose container, set cycle,
  practice, read the report, adjust, and check life effects. It explicitly
  discourages page coverage, status comparison, harder intensity, continuous
  technique, and private proof as planning goals.
- `public-atlas/retreat-and-aftercare.md` - routes retreat, long-sit, and
  intense-practice aftereffects through re-entry basics, sensory reporting,
  lighter one-week containers, feedback, behavior/service checks, and
  support-first stop signals. It separates afterglow from proof and
  aftershock from failure.
- `public-atlas/behavior-change-escalation.md` - routes insight without
  changed conduct through specific behavior naming, sensory urge practice,
  commitments, feedback, outside accountability, repair, support, and
  safety/protection gates. It preserves the claim that urge deconstruction
  can help while making ordinary structure part of the path when behavior
  keeps repeating.
- `public-atlas/source-and-claim-tiers.md` plus
  `public-atlas/how-to-read-this-site.md` - now make the atlas's source
  families, compact source-trail style, citation posture, and quotation
  posture public. This resolves the launch-trust gap without adding another
  page or exposing internal audit machinery.
- `public-atlas/asking-for-guidance.md` - turns teacher/helper conversation
  prep into a public guide: bring a bounded report, ask for a practice
  hypothesis rather than a pronouncement, clarify role and limits, preserve
  privacy and consent, watch for accountable-guidance signals, and check
  results in practice, behavior, support, and safety.

These were chosen because they move compiler value into the atlas without
adding generic Buddhist topics or another transcript-derived noun page.

## Final Gap Resolved In This Audit

### Teacher/Helper Conversation Prep

Public form: `public-atlas/asking-for-guidance.md`, supported by
`practice-report-check.md`, `guidance-scope-and-accountability.md`, and
`teacher-and-lineage.md`.

Reader job:

> "How do I ask a teacher or helper for guidance without surrendering scope,
> privacy, authority, or ordinary judgment?"

Transferred compiler value:

- report in the practitioner's own terms;
- ask for a practice hypothesis, not a pronouncement;
- clarify role and limits;
- check what happens;
- preserve consent, privacy, feedback, independence, ordinary care, and
  referral boundaries.

Status: closed for this audit. A future pass should reopen it only if a
fresh reader-route test shows that the new guide is unclear, too thin, or
insufficiently linked.

## Claims Now Better Carried

- A life event can be a Wall or Window only after ordinary situation,
  behavior, sensory challenge, support, and safety are visible.
- A good practice report is routable, not impressive.
- Guidance should be branch-and-loop: context, branch, quality, loop.
- The same phrase in a report can route differently depending on method,
  focus range, CCE quality, life effect, and safety boundary.
- Severe life events, behavior failures, and teacher pressure are not just
  meditation material.
- A week/month practice plan should optimize for aim, container, CCE quality,
  reportability, life effects, support, and safety rather than coverage,
  advanced-sounding objects, harder intensity, or continuous technique.
- Retreat and long-sit aftereffects should route through re-entry basics,
  practice reports, feedback, behavior/service checks, and support-first
  boundaries before attainment interpretation, state chasing, or intensity
  escalation.
- Behavior that keeps repeating after insight should route through sensory
  urge practice plus concrete commitments, feedback, support structures,
  repair, and safety/protection gates before asking for more meditation.
- Public trust now has a visible source posture: source families are named,
  source trails are explained as compact provenance rather than audit
  machinery, paraphrase is the default, long quotation waits for
  rights/publication policy, and claims outside practice instruction stay in
  lower tiers unless separately evidenced.
- Asking for help is now framed as a bounded practice conversation: a
  serious reader brings a routable report, asks for a testable hypothesis,
  names role limits, protects privacy and consent, watches for dependence or
  scope overreach, and checks whether the advice improves practice and life.
