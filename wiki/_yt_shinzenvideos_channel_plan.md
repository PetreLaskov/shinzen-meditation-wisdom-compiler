## Aim

Acquire a complete, provenance-rich transcript corpus for accessible spoken
videos on `@ShinzenVideos`, grouped by the channel's playlist categories, then
select what belongs in the knowledge compiler according to future
practice-reasoning value.

The central distinction:

- Acquisition asks: what exists, what transcript can be recovered, where does
  it sit in the channel/playlist structure, and how reliable is it?
- Selection asks: what should become compiled wiki evidence because it
  improves practice routing, phenomenological discrimination, transmission
  fidelity, safety/service judgment, or source calibration?

Do not ingest the whole scraped corpus blindly.

## Non-Goals

- Do not treat playlist names as Shinzen's own taxonomy unless the videos
  explicitly support that reading.
- Do not create a source page for every scraped transcript before selection.
- Do not create generic Buddhist, psychology, science, or biography pages just
  because a playlist category or title mentions them.
- Do not use the wiki as a general YouTube archive. The raw transcript corpus
  is evidence; the compiled layer should stay selective and compressed.
- Do not store audio or video downloads in the repo. Keep those in scratch
  only when needed for transcription or spot checks.

## Transcript Format

Each final transcript file should use this shape:

```markdown
# <title>

- **Video ID:** <11-char id>
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=<id>
- **Channel:** ShinzenVideos
- **Channel URL:** https://www.youtube.com/@ShinzenVideos
- **Primary playlist:** <playlist title or ungrouped>
- **Playlist memberships:** <playlist title> (<playlist id>, position <n>); ...
- **Uploader/channel reported by YouTube:** <value>
- **Upload date:** YYYY-MM-DD | unknown
- **Source:** youtube captions | youtube auto captions | faster-whisper (<model>, <device>, <compute>) | external transcript proxy
- **Length:** <duration>
- **Scrape date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Transcript quality:** A | B | C | D
- **Notes:** <caption availability, overlap, speaker attribution, silence, failures, caveats>

---

## Transcript

[00:00:00] <text when timestamps are available>
```

Preserve timestamps whenever available. For long talks, dialogues, guided
practices, and multi-speaker material, timestamps are required because they
make later source-page audit and spot checks possible.

## Quality Tiers

- **A - official or human-edited captions**: ingest-ready after light
  normalization and spot checks.
- **B - YouTube auto captions**: usable for routing and selection; exact
  wording, names, Buddhist terms, and technical vocabulary require spot
  checks before source-page citation.
- **C - local STT fallback**: usable after sample audit; source pages must
  mark exact wording and speaker attribution as transcript-limited.
- **D - no usable transcript**: inaccessible, private, deleted, silent,
  chant-only, music-only, foreign-language without useful English, or
  transcription failed.

For local STT, default to a lightweight model only for broad coverage. Use a
larger model or targeted retranscription for selected high-value items when
exact wording matters.

1. Enumerate `https://www.youtube.com/@ShinzenVideos/playlists`.
2. Record every public playlist: playlist ID, title, URL, owner/uploader,
   listed video count, description if available, and enumeration date.
3. Enumerate each playlist with `extract_flat` first. Capture video ID,
   title, duration, playlist position, uploader/channel, availability, and
   URL.
4. Enumerate `https://www.youtube.com/@ShinzenVideos/videos` as a cross-check.
   Any public channel video not found in a playlist goes into an `ungrouped`
   category in `_VIDEO_INDEX.md`.
5. Create `_CHANNEL_MANIFEST.md` and one playlist `_MANIFEST.md` per playlist
   before fetching transcripts.
6. Stop for review if playlist enumeration returns zero, YouTube blocks the
   run, the channel has unexpectedly huge scale, or the playlists are mostly
   non-Shinzen/non-teaching material.

Discovery output contract:

- Channel manifest with playlist table.
- Playlist manifests with flat video rows.
- Deduplication pre-report by video ID against the existing raw YouTube
  corpus.
- Count of unique video IDs, duplicate playlist memberships, ungrouped
  videos, and inaccessible/private/deleted items.

## Phase 1 - Transcript Acquisition

For each unique accessible video ID:

1. Check whether a canonical transcript already exists under
   `raw/Shinzen Sources/yt transcripts/`, including `retranscribed/`,
   `edited/`, root transcripts, retreat streams, and any future
   channel-specific folders.
2. If the same video ID already has a transcript, do not write a duplicate.
   Record the `@ShinzenVideos` playlist membership in the new manifests.
3. If no transcript exists, inspect full video metadata for captions.
4. Prefer official or human captions over auto captions.
5. Prefer English caption tracks. If multiple English variants exist, record
   the chosen language code and extension.
6. Fetch and normalize caption text into the final Markdown format.
7. If no usable captions exist, download audio to scratch and run local STT.
8. If STT fails or would be disproportionate, mark the video `D` with a clear
   reason and leave no transcript file.
9. Spot-check first, middle, and late samples for every transcript longer
   than 30 minutes. For high-value candidates, also spot-check dense Q&A,
   chants, names, technical terms, and quiet practice segments.
10. Update `_VIDEO_INDEX.md` and playlist manifests with status, raw path,
    quality tier, and notes.

Avoid transcript proxy services unless direct captions and local STT both
fail or are blocked. Do not use account cookies unless the user explicitly
provides them and the video is legitimately accessible to the user.

## Phase 2 - Deduplication And Overlap

Deduplicate at two levels:

- **Exact video-ID duplicate**: one canonical raw transcript only. Preserve
  new playlist membership in manifests.
- **Likely content duplicate with different video IDs**: compare title,
  duration, first/middle/last transcript shingles, and source context. If it
  is the same talk or a near-identical excerpt, mark it as a duplicate
  candidate before selection.

Same or similar title is not duplicate evidence by itself. A true duplicate
requires same video ID, same talk/session, substantially same transcript
content, or an excerpt wholly contained in an already ingested source.

Canonical transcript preference:

1. `retranscribed/` usable transcript.
2. `edited/` usable transcript.
3. Existing root or channel-specific transcript with better provenance or
   quality.
4. New `ShinzenVideos/videos/` transcript.

If the new channel provides materially better captions for an already
compiled video, do not overwrite the existing raw file. Create a deliberate
retranscription/upgrade artifact only after selection, then cite the upgraded
canonical path in any future source-page revision.

## Phase 3 - Selection

Selection happens after acquisition, not during scraping. It should produce a
durable selection report before any source-page ingestion.

Recommended artifact:

```text
wiki/_yt_shinzenvideos_selection_report.md
```

Selection unit:

- A single video when it is standalone.
- A playlist or series when sequence matters.
- A duplicate cluster when the same talk appears multiple times.
- A clip cluster when multiple short excerpts derive from a longer source.

### Selection Rubric

Score each candidate on these axes:

| Axis | Question | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shinzen-primary status | Is Shinzen the primary speaker or teacher? | 0-3 |
| Practice-routing value | Does it change how a future agent routes a practitioner? | 0-3 |
| Phenomenological precision | Does it sharpen what to notice in experience? | 0-3 |
| Teaching-transmission value | Does it preserve a distinctive teaching move, analogy, idiolect, or live coaching style? | 0-3 |
| Safety/service value | Does it clarify risks, boundaries, ethics, clinical limits, or behavior/service tests? | 0-3 |
| Novelty against current wiki | Does it add something not already compressed by existing owner pages? | 0-3 |
| Series/category value | Does playlist order or category create a real teaching progression? | 0-2 |
| Transcript reliability | Is the transcript good enough to support source-page claims? | 0-2 |
| Duplication penalty | Is it already covered or only an excerpt of a covered item? | 0 to -4 |

High score alone does not force ingestion. A low-score video may still be
selected if it repairs an active epistemic debt or supplies rare source
evidence for a disputed practice boundary.

### Include Strongly

Under the current policy, select Shinzen-primary practice/wisdom videos by
default when they do one or more of the following:

- Give primary Shinzen instruction not already present in the compiled
  corpus.
- Sharpen CCE, See Hear Feel, the Sensory Grid, Noting, Do Nothing, Rest,
  Flow, Gone, positive practice, or method choice.
- Preserve live coaching, Q&A, or routing moves that future guidance can use.
- Clarify safety boundaries around intensity, dissociation, void, clinical
  issues, teacher authority, behavior, service, or practice overload.
- Improve transmission fidelity by preserving Shinzen's oral teaching method,
  idiolect, analogies, humor, anti-guru posture, or translation moves.
- Supply a better canonical version of an already important talk.
- Form a real playlist-level progression that would be lost if ingested as
  disconnected individual videos.

### Defer Or Skip

Defer or skip videos only when they are:

- Exact content duplicates of existing compiled source pages: same video ID,
  same talk/session, substantially same transcript, or an excerpt wholly
  contained in an already ingested source.
- Non-Shinzen-primary, unless they materially clarify Shinzen's system.
- Pure chant, music, silence, event housekeeping, or generic promotional
  material.
- Guided practice with no Shinzen-primary instruction, distinction, or
  safety/routing handle.
- Low-quality transcripts whose likely practice/wisdom value does not justify
  re-auditing or retranscription.
- Broad biographical, institutional, promotional, or testimonial material
  that does not affect Shinzen's practice or wisdom system.

Do not skip or defer merely because an item has a similar title, overlaps an
existing owner page, is short, or has a stronger parallel source. If it is a
different Shinzen teaching with practice/wisdom content, ingest it or place it
in `practice-wisdom-backlog`.

### Selection Outcomes

Each video or cluster gets one status:

- **ingest-now**: belongs in the next implementation gate.
- **practice-wisdom-backlog**: in-scope Shinzen-primary practice/wisdom item
  awaiting source-page ingest or content-level reclassification.
- **series-candidate**: ingest only with its playlist/series context.
- **upgrade-existing**: use the new transcript to improve an existing source
  page or raw canonical path.
- **defer-query-driven**: deprecated for this channel unless future user
  instruction explicitly restores query-driven deferral.
- **skip-manifest-only**: keep only manifest evidence; no source page.
- **audit-needed**: transcript quality or duplicate status must be resolved
  before selection.

If many non-selected transcripts remain in `raw/`, do not let lint become a
permanent undifferentiated backlog warning. Create or update a small
selection/skip catalog and, if needed, update `tools/wiki_lint.py` to read
that catalog rather than expanding hardcoded skipped video IDs.

## Phase 4 - Ingestion Planning

After selection, create a separate implementation plan:

```text
wiki/_yt_shinzenvideos_ingestion_plan.md
```

This ingestion plan should be ordered by current-model leverage, not by
playlist order. Playlist order matters only when it carries a genuine
teaching progression.

For each gate or wave:

1. Re-read the selected raw transcript(s), existing owner pages, and relevant
   safety/service pages.
2. Create exactly one source page per ingested substantive video.
3. Update existing owner pages before creating new pages.
4. Create a playlist/series synthesis only if the sequence has a real
   through-line that individual source pages would lose.
5. Update `wiki/index.md` only for routing-relevant changes.
6. Append one `wiki/log.md` entry for the wave.
7. Run `tools\wiki_lint.cmd`.
8. Decide whether `[[Current Model]]` changed.

Pilot requirement: ingest one high-value, transcript-reliable selected item
before scheduling a large wave. Use the pilot to test whether the new channel
material mostly deepens existing owner pages, warrants new source pages, or
requires a category-level synthesis.

## Automation Contract

The automation built from this plan should run as separate stages:

1. **discover**: enumerate channel, playlists, and videos; write manifests.
2. **acquire**: fetch captions/STT and write final transcript files.
3. **dedupe**: compare against existing raw and compiled source pages.
4. **select**: produce the selection report with statuses and rationale.
5. **plan-ingest**: create the post-selection ingestion plan.
6. **ingest**: only after the user approves or the automation target clearly
   specifies the selected gate.

Each stage should be idempotent by video ID and playlist ID. Reruns should
update manifests, not duplicate transcript files.

The automation should stop and report instead of guessing when:

- The channel or playlist page cannot be enumerated.
- YouTube blocks metadata/caption access.
- A dependency install, audio download, or networked transcription step needs
  approval.
- More than 20 percent of candidates require local STT.
- A playlist is mostly non-Shinzen, private, deleted, or unavailable.
- Duplicate overlap with the existing corpus is high enough that scraping all
  remaining transcripts may be wasteful.
- Transcript quality is too weak for selection.

## Validation

Acquisition validation:

- Manifests exist and counts reconcile: playlists, playlist entries, unique
  video IDs, existing duplicates, written transcripts, STT-needed, failed,
  skipped.
- Every final transcript filename ends in `_<video_id>.md`.
- Every transcript has required metadata and a quality tier.
- Existing local transcripts are not duplicated by video ID.
- Scratch contains intermediates; repo raw contains only curated Markdown and
  manifests.

Selection validation:

- Every unique video ID has a selection status or an explicit audit-needed
  status.
- Every selected item has a reason tied to practice reasoning, transmission,
  safety/service, source calibration, or model novelty.
- Every skipped item has a concise non-ingestion reason.
- Playlist categories are preserved in the report without being mistaken for
  Shinzen's doctrinal categories.
- The next ingestion gate is small enough to complete with source pages,
  owner updates, index/log updates, and lint.

## Open Decisions For The First Automation Run

- Whether to promote all successfully scraped transcripts into `raw/`
  immediately, or first write the complete transcript cache in scratch and
  promote only after the initial selection report.
- Which STT model to use for no-caption videos before selection. A small
  model may be enough for triage; selected high-value items may need better
  retranscription.
- Whether to add a lint-readable skip/defer catalog before importing a large
  number of non-selected transcript files.
- Whether playlist categories are semantically meaningful enough to drive
  ingestion gates, or merely acquisition groupings.
