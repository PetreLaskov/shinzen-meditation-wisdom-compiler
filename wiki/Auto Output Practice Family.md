The Auto output family is Shinzen's practice cluster for detecting output
that self-organizes: body movement, speech, thought, and the optional
combination of all three. It expands the older [[Auto Move]] owner without
turning spontaneity into conduct authority.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Auto practice trains CCE around the "just happening" quality of output, not unconscious drift or impulsive expression.
- **Why this matters**: It gives future agents a clean router for body, speech, thought, and combined output practice instead of forcing all questions through the movement-specific [[Auto Move]] page.
- **Key tensions**: Self-organizing output can feel liberating, creative, playful, or Source-adjacent, but it still requires ordinary safety, social, vocal, truth, and feedback checks.
- **Source posture**: Probable from one quality-A demonstration and three quality-B shorts; exact lineage terms, religious examples, and technical phrases need spot checks before quotation.

## Model
[[Auto Walk Demonstrated by Shinzen Young]] gives the family frame. Shinzen
maps spontaneity practice onto body, speech, thought, and all-three output
and says Auto Walk means tuning into movement the body already knows how to
do, tasting its reflex-like "just happening" quality rather than inventing
special movement ([[Auto Walk Demonstrated by Shinzen Young]] S1-S2).

[[Auto Chant or Auto Speak]] supplies the speech branch. Auto Speak names
speech that self-organizes; Auto Chant trains it by using familiar vocal
material while noticing the speech organs organizing sound. Shinzen's
secular example is counting, and his boundary is translational: religious
or culturally specific material can fit some practitioners, but secular or
clinical contexts need nonreligious substitutes ([[Auto Chant or Auto Speak]]
S1-S3).

[[Auto Think]] supplies the thought branch. The entry is not vague
inspiration; it begins with inner sensory tracking: Image, Talk, Both, or
Rest. If the practitioner can cover mind space and a global unfixed state
opens, thought may self-organize as creative or insightful. If not, the
method loops back to simpler mental-state noting ([[Auto Think]] S2-S4).

[[Auto Everything]] confirms the cluster. Shinzen names four standard
techniques: Auto Move/Walk, Auto Speak/Chant, Auto Think, and Auto
Everything. The combined version samples body movement, chant, and mind-space
coverage together, after work with individual branches ([[Auto Everything]]
S1-S4).

The family also clarifies the older output-side Source language. [[Auto
Move]] remains the best owner for the movement branch and its retreat-stream
safety frame; this page owns the broader body/speech/thought taxonomy.

## Practice Use
Route by output branch:

- **Body**: use [[Auto Move]] or Auto Walk when safe movement can happen while automaticity is detected with CCE.
- **Speech**: use Auto Chant with familiar material when vocal strain, social context, and belief-context are workable.
- **Thought**: use Auto Think after Image/Talk/Both/Rest tracking is stable enough to support mind-space coverage.
- **Combined**: use Auto Everything only when the separate branches are workable and the setting can tolerate playful, divided-output practice.

In all branches, the practice target is detected self-organization. If the
person is merely lost, performative, disinhibited, compulsive, or unable to
stop, the Auto label is not helping.

## Boundaries
Auto output is not impulse permission. Spontaneous-feeling movement, speech,
thought, creativity, or teaching still needs ordinary checks: safety,
consent, role clarity, truth testing, feedback, vocal and physical capacity,
and task competence.

Auto speech is not automatic revelation. [[Auto Chant or Auto Speak]] honors
automatic contour while explicitly not endorsing overstrong content claims
about speech being grammatical human language. Use the same caution for
messages, advice, diagnoses, or teaching that arise automatically.

Auto thought is not automatic truth. [[Auto Think]] links self-organizing
thought with creativity and insight, but the output still needs review
before it guides action or claims knowledge.

Auto Everything increases method load. Combining movement, speech, and mind
coverage can be playful or integrative, but it is contraindicated when it
splits attention away from driving, machinery, caregiving, conflict,
professional judgment, unstable terrain, social consent, or ordinary
responsiveness.

## Implications
The Auto family gives Shinzen's no-self and Source-expression language a
concrete output-side grammar. Future agents should ask which output branch is
active, whether CCE is actually present, and whether ordinary action checks
remain intact.

It also improves owner-page clarity: [[Auto Move]] can stay movement-specific,
while this page routes broader questions about Auto Speak, Auto Think, and
Auto Everything.
