Surface To Source names Shinzen's stepwise route from ordinary self/world experience into Source language. It is not a separate technique; it is a routing sequence that passes through complete sensory experience, no-self untangling, Flow, and Gone, then returns to ordinary improvement and service as the maturity test.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Ordinary or restful sensory untangling can weaken self's thingness, deeper untangling can reveal Flow, vanishings can point toward Source, and mature practice must also improve self and world.
- **Why this matters**: The page prevents Source from floating free as metaphysics by keeping it tied to manageable sensory procedures and life/service accountability.
- **Key tensions**: The Source side uses broad mystic and divine-nothing language, while the improvement side lacks behavioral verification criteria.
- **Source posture**: Probable as a synthesis of Shinzen's Source route; strongest in the short Gate 4 talk and Science of Enlightenment chapter 10, with comparative and metaphysical claims source-attributed.

## Model
The compact sequence comes from [[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]]. First, ordinary applied mindfulness separates Feel/Image/Talk strands and weakens the felt sense that there is a limited thing called a self. If that ordinary mode is harsh, restful states can carry the same insight and purification sequence in a more pleasant modality ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S1-S2).

Second, the separated strands themselves can be seen as vibration-like energy. Shinzen identifies this with Focus on Flow and insight into impermanence ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S3). This makes [[Flow]] the deeper untangling of the same sensory strands rather than a separate spiritual substance.

Third, vibrations can be tracked as disappearing. Watching those vanishings routes to [[Gone]] and points back toward the nothing or Source that Shinzen translates through broad mystic language ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S4-S5). The safer owner-page boundary still applies: [[Source And Polarities]] treats Source as known through vanishing and aftereffect, not as another ordinary sensory object.

[[World Mysticism with a Capital M]] gives the comparative vocabulary for the
same route. Shinzen describes capital-M mysticism as movement from surface to
Source, distinguishes small-m intermediate-realm phenomena from Source/no-self
paradigm shift, and translates Yoga's nirbija samadhi, Buddhist
Gone/emptiness, and Heart Sutra form/emptiness into the same surface-to-Source
cycle ([[World Mysticism with a Capital M]] S3, S6-S10).

[[Journey to True Spirituality]] gives the whole-person version of the route.
Surface-to-Source practice is not merely an inward ladder: it passes through
personality feedback, depth material, the Image/Talk/Feel inner system,
behavior urges, elastic identity, Source, and the ability to meet others as
Source-loved before ordinary judgment arises ([[Journey to True Spirituality]]
S3-S10).

Science of Enlightenment chapter 10 supplies the larger map. Surface-to-Source practice can move from solid sensation through undulation, vibration, simultaneous Expansion-Contraction, cancellation, tranquility, and repolarization, while also carrying bhanga risk and afterglow limits ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S10, S14-S19).

## Gold Standard
The Gate 4 talk adds the governing maturity criterion: there are two path principles, getting over self and world through complete sensory experience of self and world, and improving self and world. Shinzen calls their mutual reinforcement the gold standard for spiritual maturity ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S6-S7).

This makes Surface To Source a polarity, not an escape route. The transcendence side belongs with [[Complete Experience]], [[No-Self And Personality]], [[Flow]], [[Gone]], and [[Source And Polarities]]. The improvement side belongs with [[Total Happiness]], behavior change, service, and guidance/accountability boundaries.

[[How the Endeavor of Improve Supports Transcend]] makes the polarity
practice-routable. When formal practice is "spinning wheels," Shinzen says
ordinary improvement or service, such as volunteering, may make the sit move
again. He generalizes this as the way improving self/world supports seeing
beyond self/world: ethics, lifestyle, diet, exercise, reduced conflict, and
knowing one is putting good into the world can become background supports for
formal transcend practice ([[How the Endeavor of Improve Supports Transcend]]
S1-S5).

[[Shinzen, the Mindful Math Geek]] gives an applied competence case. Shinzen
describes learning math after monastic training so he could participate in a
future meditation/science dialogue, using concentration, Image/Talk/Feel
deconstruction, and role-model merging to work through a formerly feared
domain. The source matters for this page because the improve branch is tested
by ordinary performance: either the math problems can be solved or they cannot
([[Shinzen, the Mindful Math Geek]] S2-S6).

## Boundaries
Surface To Source should not be read as a mandatory attainment ladder. The short talk compresses a path, but it does not say every practitioner must pass through the same phenomenology or vocabulary.

It should not be used to bypass ordinary repair. Shinzen's own gold standard requires improvement of self and world, so Source language must remain accountable to behavior, service, support, and feedback.

The true-spirituality talk makes this boundary concrete: if years of practice
leave behavior unchanged and people around the practitioner still report the
same problem, that mismatch should matter and may require a specialist rather
than more transcendence language ([[Journey to True Spirituality]] S3, S6).

It should also not turn improvement into moral pressure. The improve branch
supports transcend when it reduces conflict, repairs conditions, or brings
service into alignment; it does not prove that every stuck sit is caused by
ethical failure or that service obligations should override rest, clinical
care, consent, or practical limits ([[How the Endeavor of Improve Supports
Transcend]] S1-S5).

It should not be used as clinical guidance. The route includes no-self, Flow, Gone, and Source territory that can destabilize some practitioners; risk cases still route through [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] and its child pages.

## Implications
Future agents can use Surface To Source as a mid-level route when a query asks how Shinzen connects ordinary sensory work to advanced Source language. The preferred explanation should move in order: complete sensory experience of ordinary or restful self/world, no-self untangling, Flow, Gone, Source afterglow, and improvement/service verification.
