This retreat-stream transcript records a six-hour Shinzen daylong at the
Monastic Academy. Its durable contribution is a whole-day practice scaffold:
apply four training themes to body experience, carry one technique through
stillness and motion, then close with explicit life and driving caution.

## Teaching Register
Long retreat instruction with guided practice, Q&A, lineage translation, and
closing safety. Shinzen gives enough theory to orient the day, then treats
discussion, walking, posture shifts, and driving as practice-choice moments.

## Why This Talk Matters
The talk does not create a standalone four-quadrant system. It shows how
Shinzen can organize a daylong by applying four existing directions to one
modality: appreciate body, transcend through body spaciousness, express
through spontaneous movement, and nurture positive body emotion.

## Key Claims
- **S1**: Shinzen frames the day as unbroken practice across stillness, motion, presentation, and discussion; discussion can continue as formal practice, background practice, micro-hits, or deliberate suspension.
- **S2**: The retreat's four training themes are appreciate, transcend, express, and nurture: ordinary sensory appreciation, Source-facing transcendence, action through body/speech/thinking, and deliberate positive inner content.
- **S3**: Mindful awareness is taught as concentration, sensory clarity, and equanimity; concentration can be broad or momentary, clarity includes untangling and subtle detection, and equanimity is noninterference with sensory experience rather than indifference to conditions.
- **S4**: Practice is applied to relief of suffering, fulfillment from pleasure, deep self-knowledge, behavior change, and love or service.
- **S5**: Cultivation has two sides: developing skills or states and realizing that nothing needs to be developed; Shinzen rejects making either side the whole path.
- **S6**: Noting body locations trains spatial resolution by letting attention be drawn to body regions, including whole body or beyond body, while using approximate labels and welcoming pleasant, unpleasant, both, or neither.
- **S7**: The Feel In/Rest Q&A defines emotional body sensation operationally by whether it seems obviously emotional to a given person at a given time; the category is mainly for detection, because subtle undetected body emotion can distort perception and behavior.
- **S8**: When body-location practice mostly reveals discomfort, Shinzen gives options: switch to a more rewarding legitimate technique, add equanimity supports, or stay with discomfort as purification taste, while explicitly not damaging the body.
- **S9**: Auto Move trains the Express quadrant by letting walking or body motion be experienced as happening on its own; Shinzen links this to Zen/Chan spontaneity and says it can generalize toward speech and thought.
- **S10**: Feel Space is taught through body rest and spaciousness: openness around the body, thinness through the body, or both at once.
- **S11**: Nurture Positive is taught as positive body emotion supported by restful/flowing body states, positive Image, positive Talk, direct activation, or a physical smile, with background equanimity when nonpositive material arises.
- **S12**: The closing transition treats post-retreat life as safety-sensitive; driving practice is restricted to sensory attention to driving rather than altered, inward, or Source-facing practice.

## Summary
Shinzen makes the six-hour period one continuous practice field. Stillness,
motion, presentation, and discussion can all be practice, but only when the
mode is explicit: formal practice, background practice, micro-hits, or
deliberate suspension.

The theory is broad but quickly narrowed. Mindfulness is CCE applied to
suffering relief, fulfillment, self-knowledge, behavior change, and service;
the retreat applies appreciate, transcend, express, and nurture to body
experience. Appreciate becomes body-location noting, transcend becomes body
Rest and Space, express becomes Auto Move, and nurture becomes Feel Good in
the body.

The Q&A clarifies live routing. Feel In is an operational detection category,
not a demand to parse every body sensation. Discomfort-heavy practice may be
switched, supported, or used for purification taste, but not at the cost of
body damage. The closing keeps the retreat container accountable to ordinary
safety: driving should stay with sensory driving.
