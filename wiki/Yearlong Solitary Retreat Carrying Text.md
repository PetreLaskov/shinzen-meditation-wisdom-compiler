**Question:** What single document should a serious practitioner carry into a one-year silent solitary retreat with a notebook, no books, and no technology, when the document will be reread hundreds of times and must avoid theatrical wisdom?

**Answer:** The best artifact is a compact operating manual: one loop, several gates, and embedded failure clauses. It is designed to renew through use by returning the practitioner to the next sensory event, choosing a method by failure mode, and interrupting retreat pathologies before they become practice identity.

This is an analysis page, not new Shinzen evidence. Its principal raw anchors support the CCE-plus-time transformation model, life-practice cycles, retreat aftercare, void distress boundary, and Do Nothing gate; the page's actual design judgment depends mainly on compiled pages named in [[#Dependencies]].

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: For yearlong rereading under solitude and amplification, practice needs a reversible loop more than inspiring content: contact the next event, test concentration/clarity/equanimity, choose one gate, and simplify when the tests fail.
- **Why this matters**: A solitary retreat artifact can become rumination fuel, attainment theater, or a bypass script unless the document itself repeatedly returns to sensory contact, ordinary care, method switching, and behavior checks.
- **Key tensions**: The artifact is useful only as a practice governor; it is not live guidance, clinical screening, retreat permission, emergency support, or proof that solitude is appropriate.
- **Source posture**: Probable as a Shinzen-informed design synthesis from existing compiled pages; speculative as a safety framework for an actual unmonitored year.

## Design Rationale
The selected form is a compact operating manual: one loop, several gates, and embedded failure clauses. A question list would invite rumination, koans would invite performance, a calendar would become brittle, and a long essay would go dead. A loop renews because it must be enacted against what is happening now.

The invented selection metric is **reversible traction**: each line must give contact, diagnosis, and a way to change course when it starts harming. This metric favors handles that can be used in clarity, despair, fear, tedium, altered states, and the middle months. These choices are weakest under the metric of immediate comfort because they are not mainly soothing.

The structural revision cut the month-by-month ladder because it would become progress theater. It cut elegant riddles because they would reward interpretation over practice. It reshaped safety from a separate warning section into gates inside the loop.

## Carried Document

Do not improve the year. Complete the next event.

An event is enough when it can be known as sight, sound, touch, image, talk, body-emotion, rest, change, or vanishing. If it cannot be known that way, do not believe it yet.

The three tests are: Can this be stayed with? Can its parts be distinguished? Can it move without extra push or pull? If one test fails, train that one. If all fail, reduce the load.

Use one gate at a time.

1. **Outer gate**: sights, sounds, touch. Use when lost, inflated, vague, frightened, or too inward.
2. **Inner gate**: image, talk, body-emotion. Use when stories multiply or old material drives the body.
3. **Rest gate**: relaxation, silence, blankness, peace. Use when strained, ill, exhausted, or overworked.
4. **Change gate**: flow, vibration, spread, contraction, fading, Gone. Use only when change is actually detectable.
5. **Goodness gate**: one wholesome phrase, image, or body tone. Use after void, flatness, harshness, or cold clarity.
6. **No-doing gate**: when the impulse to manage attention is noticed, drop that impulse. If this turns dull or drifty, return to labels.

If too wide, narrow. If too tight, soften. If too dull, label. If too racy, stop managing. If too much, turn away while allowing the hard thing in the background. If avoiding, turn toward the weakest workable piece.

Pain is not purification. Equanimity is the purifier. Intensity earns nothing by itself.

Blank is not automatically deep. Check: posture alive, details available, ordinary care intact. If not, use outer gate, sleep, eat, walk, clean.

Fear after a deep state is not failure. Work with the fear as body-emotion, image, and talk. If that cannot be done, reduce practice and get help.

A grand conclusion must pass through three sieves: sensory parts, ordinary functioning, and later behavior. If it fails any sieve, write "unsupported" and return to the gate.

Every day, do stillness, motion, and life. In stillness, use one gate cleanly. In motion, keep the body safe. In life, do brief hits during eating, washing, walking, lying down, waking.

The notebook is not for philosophy. Record only: strongest pattern, sensory parts, branch chosen, effect on body and conduct, unsupported claims.

When the middle goes dead, do not decorate it. Boredom, flatness, repetition, and disgust are events. Know their parts. Use outer gate often. Keep the room, body, food, and schedule sane.

Break the frame if there is urge toward bodily harm, inability to sleep for days, inability to care for the body, command-like experiences, severe disorientation, terrifying unreality, or certainty that ordinary care no longer applies. Get human help. This is practice, not defeat.

At the end of each week ask: less driven or more driven? clearer or vaguer? kinder in action or merely calmer? more embodied or more absent? If worse across the week, simplify.

At the end of each month ask: what am I now using practice to avoid? Then practice with that answer's image, talk, and body-emotion.

Do not become loyal to any state. Become reliable in returning.

## Appendix
**Selection metric:** Reversible traction: each included item must open direct practice and contain its own course-correction.

**Ambiguities that shaped the answer:**
- If the retreat has outside support, the document can lean harder into intensity; if not, it must bias toward simplification.
- If the practitioner has prior destabilization history, the red gates should be treated as earlier stop signs.
- If the practitioner is already very advanced, the ordinary-care clauses may feel blunt, but they are there for the middle of the retreat, not for the opening enthusiasm.

**Honest limit:** This document cannot replace competent human support when the retreat stops being workable.

## Confidence And Limits
The artifact is high-confidence as a compact expression of the wiki's practice-routing posture: CCE first, method fit by live failure mode, intensity never as proof, altered phenomena routed through sensory decomposition, and behavior/support as verification. It is lower-confidence as a stand-alone retreat safety plan because the wiki lacks professional-grade criteria for psychiatric vulnerability, trauma history, medical readiness, sleep risk, and the practical conditions of the retreat.

Future agents should not treat the page as permission for a yearlong solitary retreat. Treat it as an answer to a design problem after the retreat frame has been set, and route actual risk reports through [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] and [[Guidance Scope and Accountability Boundary]] first.
