Chapter 6 of the Basic Mindfulness manual is a routing compression. It summarizes the first five Ways as life strategies and ways of knowing oneself, then places them beside the three techniques and current grid of focus options.

## Key Claims
- **S1**: The Way of Thoughts and Emotions is summarized as "Untangle & Be Free"; the Way of Physical Senses as "Anchor & Merge"; the Way of Tranquility as "Refresh & Release"; the Way of Flow as "Dissolve & Digest"; and the Way of Human Goodness as "Love & Serve."
- **S2**: The same five Ways are also framed as five self-knowledge routes: know oneself as a sensory system, as Another, as Serenity, as Spirit Energy, and as the best one can be.
- **S3**: The chapter's chart places the first four Ways in the sensory grid through See/Hear/Feel and Focus options, while Human Goodness is organized by positive themes and image/talk/body modality choices.
- **S4**: The chart positions Noting, Do Nothing, and Nurture Positive as the three named techniques used across the manual's practice options.

## Summary
This short chapter does not substantially expand the instructions from chapters 1-5. Its main contribution is compression. Each Way receives two mnemonic descriptions: one practical life-strategy slogan and one self-knowledge slogan.

The chapter then gives a visual overview of the three techniques and five Ways together. The first four Ways remain Noting-based routes through inner activity, physical senses, restful states, and Flow states. The Way of Human Goodness remains the Nurture Positive route through positive themes and inner image, talk, and emotional body systems. Do Nothing appears as the non-Noting technique associated with rest/release.

## Source Frame
The source wants the reader to remember the architecture rather than learn a new practice. Its slogans are useful because they expose the manual's intended functions: untangling, anchoring, refreshing, dissolving, and serving.

The source also compresses heavily. Phrases such as "Spirit Energy," "Another," and "the best you can be" are interpretive handles, not definitions. They should route future readers back to the owning Way pages rather than carry claims by themselves.
