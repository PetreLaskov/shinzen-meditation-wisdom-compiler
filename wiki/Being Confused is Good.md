## Key Claims
- **S1**: Shinzen says there is nothing wrong with being confused; confusion is even framed as useful rather than something the practitioner must avoid.
- **S2**: The target problem is habitual coagulation around don't-know, not confusion, doubt, disorientation, or don't-know itself.
- **S3**: Uncoagulated don't-know is named as dynamic spontaneity when expressed through the body.
- **S4**: The same uncoagulated don't-know is named as wisdom when it turns in the mind.

## Load-Bearing Teaching Moves
- **S1-S2**: The talk prevents Don't Know practice from becoming an anti-confusion project. What matters is whether the not-knowing hardens, fixates, or contracts.
- **S3**: The body-side formulation gives a concise source anchor for spontaneous output language while still needing the ordinary Auto Move and conduct boundaries.
- **S4**: The mind-side formulation compresses the longer wisdom-function teaching: wisdom is knowing after the compulsion around not-knowing relaxes, not a rejection of thinking.

## Practice Handles
- When confusion appears, distinguish the bare not-knowing from the contraction, fixation, or answer-pressure around it.
- Do not make the practice goal "never be confused." The practice target is the coagulation around confusion.
- If not-knowing is linked to body spontaneity, keep movement, speech, conduct, safety, and consent boundaries active before treating spontaneity as practice.
- If not-knowing is linked to wisdom, keep ordinary inquiry and evidence available; wisdom here does not mean refusing facts or decisions.

## Summary
The clip begins by directly challenging the aversion to confusion. Shinzen
frames confusion, doubt, disorientation, and don't-know as acceptable practice
territory. The problem is not their presence but the habitual coagulation that
forms around them.

He then gives the source's central formula. When don't-know is uncoagulated,
its body-side expression is dynamic spontaneity, and its mind-side expression
is wisdom. The clip does not unpack the mechanism, but it gives a compact
phrase future agents can route through the longer Don't Know, Do Nothing, and
Auto Move pages.
