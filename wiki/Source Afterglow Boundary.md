The Source afterglow boundary is the main precision rule for Shinzen's
Source language. It lets future agents use Zero, Gone, cessation, witness,
and no-self rhetoric without making Source into a directly perceived thing,
ultimate observer, or blank state to seek.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Gone can point toward Source or Zero, but Source itself is
  not a sensory datum; the knowable side is an after-representation that
  appears when ordinary sensory processing returns.
- **Why this matters**: Without this boundary, Source language can become
  metaphysical overclaim, attainment certification, dissociative blankness,
  or a subtle self called consciousness.
- **Key tensions**: Shinzen sometimes uses strong direct-contact language in
  earlier or oral contexts, while chapter 10 gives the stricter afterglow
  calibration that should govern ordinary synthesis.
- **Source posture**: Probable as Shinzen's internal calibration; not proof
  of an objective Source outside sensory experience.

## Model

Science of Enlightenment chapter 9 says ordinary Gones point toward the
Uncreated or Source, but it also states that Source is not a sensory
experience. The sensory side is the aftereffect of vanishing becoming clearer
in the circuits ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 9 - The Power of Gone]]
S9). [[Abrupt Flow Diminishings, Vanishings and Noting Gone]] gives the oral
practice version: repeated ordinary Gones advertise attention toward
"something" that is not a thing, contacted through the senses but not
experienced as a sensory object; what becomes defined is the aftereffect
([[Abrupt Flow Diminishings, Vanishings and Noting Gone]] S4-S5).

Chapter 10 gives the strictest formulation. Shinzen says each sensory or
motor event is born from simultaneous Expansion-Contraction and that Gone is
the mutual cancellation of those polar forces into Zero ([[Science of
Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S2-S4, S12-S13). In that
cancellation there is no self present to know Source; the next instant
supplies an after-representation composed of image, talk, feeling,
fulfillment, safety, peace, and love. Enlightened wisdom is therefore a
conditioned sensory representation of the unconditioned, not the unconditioned
itself ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]]
S14-S15).

The no-self and witness sources should be read through the same rule. Science
of Enlightenment chapter 8 describes self as born from pure space through
simultaneous Expansion-Contraction and dying through Gone, while
[[A Deeper Freedom Experiences of Selflessness]] emphasizes re-arising as
unblocked personality rather than permanent absence ([[Science of
Enlightenment Chapter 8 - The Real No Self]] S18; [[A Deeper Freedom
Experiences of Selflessness]] S1-S5). [[The Absolute Witness]] says the
relative witness is still subtle Feel/Image/Talk, while the absolute witness
is contentless consciousness, Zero, nirodha, Source, emptiness, or cessation
exposed at disappearance; this is not a new observer behind experience
([[The Absolute Witness]] S2-S7).

The consciousness-release sources add a nearby caution. [[Five Aspects of the
Five Ways - 3 of 4]] treats identification with consciousness itself as a
possible final holding-on and uses the candle story to teach letting that
light go out. That is an advanced release image, not a general instruction to
seek blankness, suppress awareness, or disregard functioning when void or
dissociative states appear ([[Five Aspects of the Five Ways - 3 of 4]] S7).

## Boundaries

This boundary should override stronger direct-contact phrasing unless the
question specifically asks about that source's rhetoric. Chapter 7's
surface/intermediate/Source map and some oral talks use more direct Source
language, but chapter 10 is the better synthesis rule for direct-object
claims ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 7 - The Realm of Power]] S2-S3;
[[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S14-S15).
[[God's Arrow]] is a clear example: Shinzen speaks as if Source creates a
channel through time-space and part of him looks through it into void, but the
talk's spatial immediacy should be read as advanced oral metaphor and
after-representation rather than proof that Source is a sensory object owned
by a self ([[God's Arrow]] S1-S3).

It should not be used to trivialize meaningful reports. Afterglow can include
fulfillment, safety, peace, love, transparency, and reorganization; the
boundary says those are conditioned representations, not worthless illusions.

It also should not become clinical advice. Fear, paralysis, hopelessness,
void distress, DPDR-like flattening, harsh bhanga, sleep disruption, or
behavior worsening route through [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] and
[[Altered Phenomena and Dissolution Safety Boundary]] before Source
interpretation.
