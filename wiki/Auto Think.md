## Summary
Shinzen says Auto Think trains "thinking without thinking." The brief
procedure begins with a simple mental-state inventory: images, talk, both, or
rest. Rest can be relative, as blank screen and quiet head, or absolute, as a
collapse of image and talk space.

After this basic tracking, the practitioner may try to cover mind space,
placing attention in image space and talk space together. If that field
becomes globally unfixed, it may evolve into thinking that happens by itself:
creative, insightful, and not owned by a felt thinker. The algorithm is
explicitly reversible: if the window does not open or mind-space coverage is
too difficult, return to noting mind states.
