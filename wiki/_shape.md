# Shape Paragraph

The shape paragraph opens `wiki/index.md`. It is the wiki's first routing
surface: scope, through-line, maturity, and next leverage in three to five
sentences.

## Required Moves

1. **Scope**: what the wiki covers and what it excludes.
2. **Through-line**: the central claim or the honest absence of one.
3. **Maturity**: source coverage, synthesis stage, review state, or other
   concrete indicators.
4. **Next leverage**: the specific move that would make the wiki more useful.

Cut any sentence that does none of these.

## Skeleton

```text
This wiki tracks {domain}, with particular attention to {narrowing}; it
excludes {boundary}. The current through-line is that {claim}, though
{qualification or contested area}. The wiki is at {lifecycle stage}, with
{concrete count or indicator}. The highest-leverage next step is {specific
source, synthesis, question, review, or refactor}.
```

If no through-line exists yet, say so:

```text
No through-line has emerged yet; current pages share {domain coverage} but
do not support a durable synthesis.
```

## Anti-Patterns

- Aspirational scope: describes what the wiki may become, not what it is.
- Topic-label scope: names a subject without a claim or frame.
- Hedge-stacked through-line: hides uncertainty instead of naming it.
- Decorative maturity: says the wiki has many pages without counts or stage.
- Vague next leverage: says "continue ingesting" without naming a source or
  frontier.

## Rewrite Triggers

- After the first few substantive sources.
- During every review.
- After a major synthesis.
- After a domain refactor.
- When the dashboard or recent log makes the paragraph stale.
