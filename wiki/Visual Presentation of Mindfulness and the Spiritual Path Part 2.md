of mindfulness and the spiritual path. It overlaps strongly with the Basic
Mindfulness Big Picture chapter, but it preserves the live teaching frame:
the chart is a storage-and-retrieval mandala for "everything important" in
his system, from basic practice questions through deep polarity and broad
cosmological analogy.

## Teaching Register

The register is visual synthesis, transmission, and speculative broad map.
Shinzen is not teaching a single technique; he is showing how his whole
system can be stored in a symbolic mandala that functions as psychogram,
cosmogram, hodogram, idiogram, and memory palace.

## Why This Talk Matters

The talk strengthens the "visual sutra" reading of Basic Mindfulness. It
shows that the diagram is meant to route questions, not merely decorate a
manual: what mindfulness is, why to practice, how to practice, where it
applies, when it developed, how details and deep views are stored, and how
Expansion-Contraction becomes a broad analogy across nature and mathematics.

## Practice Handles

Use the five-question frame to orient a lost practitioner: what is being
trained, why it matters, how to practice, where it applies in the human
condition, and how the tradition or current research context frames it. Use
the deep-game frame to prevent method absolutism: work with parts and wholes,
make distinctions and unifications, use active and passive stances, and
allow self/no-self modes to complement rather than cancel each other.

## Summary

Shinzen begins by saying he will put his "three utterances" together in one
diagram that stores the essence of everything he has taught. The basic layer
answers five questions: what mindfulness is, why to practice, how to
develop it, where it applies, and when mindfulness developed or continues to
develop. The answers point to CCE, Total Happiness, technique and daily/yearly
practice, the human condition, and the historical arc from shamanism through
meditative traditions to present research.

He then describes the detailed layer. Details about what mindfulness is are
stored in the three CCE skill areas; details about why to practice are stored
in the Total Happiness chart; details about how to practice are stored in
the game boards; and the boards can be used as strategies, sensory
classification, issue-specific applications, and a historical toolkit. The
talk includes a short happiness-chart recap that duplicates the separate
3:51 `HAPPINESS CHART` clip.

The deep and broad layers widen the frame. The deep practice game involves
parts and wholes, distinctions and unifications, active and passive stances,
and self/no-self complementarity. The deep view of sensory experience
involves subjective/objective activation, Expansion-Contraction, pure spirit
spread/collapse, and rising/passing. The broad view applies the same
contrast-and-cancellation theme across cosmology, planets, organisms, cells,
atoms, and mathematical operations. Shinzen ends by paraphrasing J. B. S.
Haldane: God has an "inordinate fondness" for contrast and cancellation.

## Source Frame

The talk wants the listener to treat Shinzen's system as a symbolic
knowledge architecture. Its strongest evidence value is for how Shinzen
organizes and transmits his own map. Its weakest value is the broad
science/math/cosmology analogy, which is visually and verbally evocative but
not evidence that nature or mathematics objectively validate Source or
Expansion-Contraction.
