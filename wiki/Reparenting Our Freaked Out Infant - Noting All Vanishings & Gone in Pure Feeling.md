## Practice Handles
- When Gone practice becomes agitating, do not assume the cause. Check whether the agitation tracks the vanishing practice or whether sleep, stimulants, life stress, or other factors may be involved.
- Distinguish the agitation's dominant channel: Feel, Image, or Talk. If it is primarily Feel, the route is emotional-body practice rather than explanation-chasing.
- If capacity is present, continue noting vanishings while including the Feel vanishings, not only breath endings or external sounds.
- Pace the process. Sitting and napping can both be legitimate; ego-death-like work should happen in manageable doses.
- Keep clinical and support boundaries active. A Source/reparenting interpretation does not rule out panic, trauma activation, dissociation, sleep disturbance, or functional destabilization.

## Summary
A practitioner reports that noting vanishings, previously soothing when done with breath and sound endings, has become global and agitating. Shinzen asks whether the agitation is actually correlated with paying attention to vanishing and whether the agitation is mainly Feel, Image, or Talk. Once the practitioner confirms a strong correlation and a primarily Feel-based agitation, Shinzen says the technique has been done well rather than badly.

His interpretation is that vanishing points to the most primordial nonhuman groundless grounding and then to the next most primitive human layer: chaotic infant-like Feel. The adult meditator can now include that Feel and its vanishings in practice, working through the primal freak-out that he says underlies later adult and adolescent freak-outs.

Shinzen then gives the Sasaki-linked polarity model: Zero breaks into Expansion and Contraction, creates a cleft of space, and vibrates into pure heat of Feel. Detecting and completing that Feel lets it resolve back into Expansion, Contraction, their cancellation, and Gone. He calls this reparenting the freaked-out infant by its cosmic parents, but closes with a pacing cue: the practitioner may sit or nap, and ego death should be taken in manageable doses.
