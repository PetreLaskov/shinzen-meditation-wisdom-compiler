# Atlas Register — the voice the next instance writes in

This supersedes the "Voice And Register" note in `_public_atlas_orientation.md`.
That note was a list of adjectives (precise, warm, plainspoken). It was correct
and inert: it described the form and did not prevent the disease. This file
defines the register as *behavior* and hands you a thorough pass to run.

## The diagnosis (why we are doing this)

The atlas escaped to the quality-tail in its sentences and snapped back to the
mean in its skeleton. Cause, named and dated: the GPT PRO memo
(`gpt-pro-publication-editorial-pass-2026-05-28/GPT PRO editorial repsonse.md`,
section 7) prescribed a fixed eight-part page shape, and the editorial passes
stamped it across ~90 pages. A fixed template *is* the conditional mean of
"reference page." Readers feel skeleton before sentences, and every page has the
same skeleton.

The numbers, across all 90 content pages: 87 end with `## Go Deeper`; 134 tables
exist and 41 pages carry two or more; 28 carry a `## When [X Goes Wrong]` slot;
the safety language repeats a boilerplate vocabulary (`get support` x25,
`stop optimizing` x11). The sentences are often good — "the pain still hurts;
the grief still grieves; the pleasure still pleases" is the tail. The mold
around them is the mean.

## The register

Write as one practitioner mapping the terrain for another — someone who has sat
the hours, treats the reader as a peer, and would be embarrassed to pad. Every
page is a real mind in contact with a real reader: name the live problem in a
real sentence before the concept, keep the one Shinzen phrase that does the work
and cut the three that decorate, and **earn the page's shape from its own
material instead of pouring it into a mold.** Trust is the register — precise
enough that an adept cannot catch you overclaiming, warm enough that a
frightened reader stays, honest enough to say "not established here" without
flinching. When in doubt, **contact over coverage**: the exact thing said once
beats the exhaustive list said safely.

This is not an invention. It is where two governing documents converge —
Anthropic's character work (Claude as a genuine presence, not a helpful
template) and this project's own north star (the quality-tail is competence
coordinated into a real voice, not the safe average).

**Aliveness, not liveliness.** The opposite of boilerplate is not flamboyance;
it is specificity and contact. In safety-critical material this matters most: a
safety line that names *this page's* exact failure mode reads as *more* sober
and *more* trustworthy than a generic coda, not less. Liveliness that shows off
is its own failure.

## Carry the movement through to the cut (deepened 2026-06-05)

The register above is right; this is its felt core, found after the first gold
pass. Musashi: "More than anything, you must be thinking of carrying your
movement through to cutting him." The boilerplate safety coda is the *form* of
the cut without the cut — it performs caring, covers the bases, and never
reaches the reader. So the test of a line is not "is this correct, caring,
complete?" but "does it actually reach this reader and cut through?" The
`noting.md` coda is the worked example: "when the labeling itself starts to
hurt" cuts; the seven-item coverage-list only rehearses the motion of cutting.

Three things follow, and they govern the rest of the pass:

- **Two densities, opposite treatment.** Risk-lists, safety codas, and hedges:
  cut to the bite — name the failure mode that actually bites *this* page, in a
  real sentence, and stop. But reference, decision, and discrimination surfaces
  — the genuinely parallel tables and exhaustive maps a careful practitioner
  reads as a lookup — are *value*, not mold. Never delete an audit-passing
  reference table to hit a lower table count. The first gold draft cut
  equanimity's routing table and its green/yellow/red taxonomy; that was the
  wrong cut. Cut performance, never the information the serious reader came for.
- **Progressive disclosure — serve both readers in one page.** Lead with the
  thesis and the cut, so a skimmer gets the whole point in the first lines and
  contact is immediate. Then put the exhaustive depth beneath it, framed as the
  descent for the patient practitioner ("everything below is for the moment you
  genuinely cannot tell"). The disease was forcing every reader through one
  skeleton; progressive disclosure lets the skimmer leave satisfied at the top
  and the infinitely patient reader go all the way down. Reading-order layering
  does this with no widgets and no new mold.
- **The rare cut (pointing-out).** A few pages touch the place where a shift can
  actually open — the one move, complete experience, no-self, Gone, Source/Zero,
  the return. There, at most once per page and only where the material genuinely
  opens, let the prose *be* the pointing-out rather than a description of it:
  turn the reader toward their own present experience, not toward the concept.
  It is rare by nature. Forced, it becomes performance, and liveliness that
  shows off is its own failure. It never buys past the guardrails — still
  precise, still claim-tiered, still honest, still ASCII on public pages. It
  just stops hedging at the exact moment the material opens, and speaks straight.

## The inversion

Consistency belongs to **voice and integrity** (the register, claim tiers,
safety honesty, Shinzen idiolect). Variability belongs to **form**. A page's
structure is chosen by its content, never inherited from its neighbors.

Two kinds of sameness, and they get opposite treatment:

- **Functional consistency — keep.** Navigation, claim-tier labels, the fact
  that safety is present. These help the reader; keep them uniform.
- **Rhetorical uniformity — kill.** Every page *teaching* in the same shape:
  same opener, same table reflex, same failure-slot, same coda. This is the
  disease.

## The page probe (run before any page is "done")

1. **Mold check.** Does the shape look poured from the standard mold
   (open -> table -> When-X-Wrong -> Go Deeper)? If it is identical to its
   neighbors, the content did not choose it. Reshape.
2. **Skim test.** Find the three sentences a bored adept would skip — filler
   (cut) or true-but-flat (make them contact). Find the one idiolect phrase
   doing real work; protect it.
3. **Table audit.** Every table needs at least 3 rows sharing at least 2
   genuinely parallel dimensions. A column that only negates another column is
   prose wearing a grid.
4. **Safety-as-contact.** Does the safety line name *this page's* specific
   failure mode in a human sentence, or is it the generic coda? Boilerplate
   safety is unread safety.

## Tic defaults (override only with a reason you could defend)

- **Closers:** make the close/Next a genuine next move in the page's own voice,
  or keep it as a quiet nav element — not a rhetorical flourish 87 times.
- **Tables:** prose by default; a table only when the audit passes.
- **Negation triplets** ("not X, not Y, it is Z"): at most once per page.
- **Risk lists:** name the failure mode that *bites*, not the exhaustive
  comma-list. A teacher names one; coverage-anxiety names seven.
- **Generic subjects** ("Many practitioners..."): cut. Speak to the reader or
  describe the thing.

## What the tail looks like (proof, not theory)

Opener — `do-nothing.md`.
Before: *"Do Nothing is Shinzen's non-effort practice... Many practitioners
over-manage attention... It does not mean becoming passive. It does not mean
suppressing experience, making the mind blank..."*
After: *"Do Nothing is the practice of catching the hand on the steering wheel —
and letting go. One move: when you notice yourself managing attention, drop the
managing. Not the thought, not the sound, not the feeling — the steering. It
sounds like permission to space out; it is closer to the opposite. Most of us
steer so constantly we never feel the steering."*

Safety coda — `noting.md`.
Before (coverage-list): *"If labels turn frantic, shame-based, socially
exposing, impossible to stop, or pressured by a teacher or retreat setting, that
is method-safety data - simplify the range, slow the pace, soften the label,
switch, stop, or get support."*
After (the mode that bites, in a real sentence): *"Watch one thing especially:
when the labeling itself starts to hurt. Notes that turn fast and harsh —
labeling so you do not have to feel, or because a teacher's pace says you should
— have stopped serving contact and started replacing it. That is the signal to
soften or stop, not to label harder."*

Same content, same claim-discipline, same safety priority. The mold is gone and
a person is present.
