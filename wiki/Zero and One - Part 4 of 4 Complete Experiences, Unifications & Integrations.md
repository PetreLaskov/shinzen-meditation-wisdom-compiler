## Teaching Register
Advanced explanatory teaching and series conclusion. Shinzen speaks less as a step-by-step coach than as a mapmaker, showing how the sensory decomposition of prior talks recombines into larger scales of oneness.

## Why This Talk Matters
The talk prevents complete experience from being understood only as pain reduction or emotional cleanup. Its main contribution is that completed sensory strands do not merely become tolerable; when they clarify as Flow, they can integrate the apparent divisions between thought and emotion, body and mind, listener and sound, seer and seen, self and world.

## Key Claims
- **S1**: The prior Strong Determination arc represents not only thought-emotion integration but whole body-mind integration when physical touch and Feel/Image/Talk become a single unified Flow.
- **S2**: Complete experience can extend to external sight: when seeing and the thoughts/emotions about seeing are both completed, the seen object and the perceiver's inner colorations can Flow together.
- **S3**: Sound and music are often easier routes into inside/outside unification because many people already experience music as energy; the harder part is experiencing the listener's thoughts and emotions as Flow too.
- **S4**: The same sound route applies to ordinary or annoying sounds when rageful reactions to sonic invasion are parsed as Feel/Image/Talk and met with CCE alongside the sound's Flow.
- **S5**: Shinzen explicitly names impermanence, Flow, and change as "the great integrator": it integrates head-heart, mind-body, inside-outside, self-world, and finally creation with Source.
- **S6**: The Source-level integration is framed as one with zero, or creation with Creator, and Shinzen calls that "living nirvana"; this is an advanced interpretive claim, not a separate sensory technique.

## Load-Bearing Teaching Moves
- **S1**: Shinzen shifts from decomposition to recombination: the same sensory categories that distinguish "it" from "me" can unify when both sides become Flow.
- **S2**: Music is used as a bridge example because the external sound side often becomes Flow before the listener side does; the practice edge is the listener's Feel/Image/Talk reaction.
- **S3**: Annoying sound becomes a stress test for the same mechanism; the problem is not only the sound but the inner reaction to sonic invasion.
- **S4**: "A 1 and a 0 at the same time" preserves the paradox: integration is oneness, but it is vacuous rather than thing-like fusion.

## Practice Handles
When working with external sights or sounds, do not only look for Flow in the object. Include the inner side: mental images, mental talk, and emotional body reactions to what is seen or heard. If both the object-side event and the perceiver-side reactions are suffused with concentration, clarity, and equanimity, Shinzen says they can Flow together.

Use music as an accessible training case without stopping there. If music can be known as Flow, then ordinary sound and annoying sound can become the same kind of practice when the listener's irritation, rage, images, and commentary are also known as Feel/Image/Talk rather than fused into "me being invaded."

## Summary
Shinzen closes the Strong Determination story by saying the eventual reward was a tangible integration of the whole body-mind self. Touch and Feel/Image/Talk, or the "it" of physical pain and the "me" of mental-emotional reaction, can become a single unified Flow.

He then widens the pattern. With sight, a complete experience of the flower and a complete experience of one's thoughts and emotions about the flower can let seer and seen Flow together. With sound, music often makes the external side easier because people already feel energy with music; the harder task is to experience the listener's reactions as Flow. The same applies to ordinary and annoying sounds, including meditation-hall breathing, stereo noise, or snoring.

The conclusion is a scale model of integration. Flow integrates head and heart, mind and body, inside and outside, and self and world. Shinzen then adds a final Source contrast: deeper than "I" versus "it" is creation versus formless Source. When one and zero are integrated, he calls that living nirvana.
