## Practice Handles
Use very familiar vocal material, such as numbers or a personally appropriate
chant, and notice the air, lips, teeth, tongue, and other speech tissues
organizing sound without a strong controller. Keep listening and ordinary
coordination available.

## Summary
Shinzen says people sometimes experience speech organizing itself, and he
calls this Auto Speak. To train it, use Auto Chant: choose familiar material,
chant out loud, and let the speech organs self-organize. For secular
settings such as clinics or schools, he suggests simple nonreligious content
like numbers rather than explicitly religious or foreign-language material.

He then interprets glossolalia as an example of automatic speech contour. The
source honors the automaticity as spirit-like or valid in contour, while
separating that from stronger claims that the sounds are actual grammatical
human languages.
