The Source science and analogy boundary keeps Shinzen's most ambitious
bridges useful without overpromoting them. It distinguishes his high
confidence in sensory practice from his speculative use of physics,
mathematics, biology, nature, time-space, cross-tradition comparison, and
Buddhist doctrine under scientific scrutiny.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Shinzen's strongest evidence is practice-phenomenological;
  nature, math, science, and time-space language function as analogy,
  research imagination, or provisional bridge unless a source gives stronger
  evidence.
- **Why this matters**: Source/polarity language can otherwise be laundered
  into claims that contemplative experience proves physics, theology,
  neuroscience, or universal metaphysics.
- **Key tensions**: Shinzen sometimes uses grand unifying rhetoric, but he
  also explicitly marks objective reality and scientific extensions as
  conjectural.
- **Source posture**: Established for Shinzen's epistemic boundary; probable
  for the wiki's routing rule; speculative for the proposed scientific
  correspondences.

## Model

[[Reality & Sensory Experience]] gives the broad epistemic rule. Shinzen says
he has high confidence in sensory experience, CCE, selfing, resistance, and
sensory transformation because those can be investigated directly in practice.
He does not claim to know what objective reality behind sensory experience is
or whether such a reality can be accessed ([[Reality & Sensory Experience]]
S1-S5).

[[How Much of Buddhism Can Survive the Scrutiny of Science]] applies that
rule to Buddhist doctrine. Shinzen says much of Buddhism can survive scrutiny
but not all of it: reincarnation remains without compelling evidence in his
view, while suffering, pleasure, impermanence, equanimity, and no-self can be
reframed as claims about directly testable practice experience
([[How Much of Buddhism Can Survive the Scrutiny of Science]] S1-S10). The
same talk then moves into Source and death-continuity language, which should
remain Source-side interpretation rather than empirical proof ([[How Much of
Buddhism Can Survive the Scrutiny of Science]] S11-S12).

Science of Enlightenment chapter 10 applies the same caution to Source. It
explicitly agrees that mystical experience does not settle objective reality
claims and marks free-energy, entropy, thermodynamics, dialectics, complex
numbers, and nature analogies as conjectural or playful speculation
([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S6). This is
the governing rule for Source-science synthesis.

The manual's Big Picture chapter supplies the main analogy set. It extends
Expansion-Contraction into life, nature, galaxies, stars, black holes,
planets, anatomy, cells, proteins, atoms, and number systems, then asks
whether natural structures resemble contemplative formulations
([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 12 - The Big Picture]] S14). This is evidence
that Shinzen thinks the analogy is suggestive, not that the analogy is
verified.

The complex-number source gives a narrower math example. Shinzen treats
stretch/squeeze/one, opposed movement/zero, and rotation/stillness as
contrast grammars and gestures toward quantum weirdness and
something-from-nothing. The same source leaves Sasaki Roshi's possible grand
theory to time and claims only that the model is useful for analyzing
consciousness moment by moment ([[Sasaki Roshi, the Complex Number System &
the Source of Love]] S1-S5).

[[Shinzen Young Retreat at the Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]] adds a retreat
version of the same caution. Shinzen uses free energy and evoked-potential
examples to imagine how sensory experience might be related to spontaneity
and pre-conscious processing, while explicitly presenting the scientific
extension as speculative teaching language ([[Shinzen Young Retreat at the
Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]] S8). Treat this as a source for Shinzen's
research imagination, not as evidence that neuroscience or physics validates
the Source model.

[[Touching the Heart]] gives a second evoked-potential example. Shinzen uses
auditory processing stations and preconscious "just before" language to make
absolute-now contact less spatially metaphorical, but the talk does not
validate Source, unstruck sound, unborn awareness, or eternal identity as
neuroscience ([[Touching the Heart]] S3, S6-S7). Use it as translation and
research imagination, not proof.

[[God's Arrow]] gives the most vivid time-space metaphor now compiled from the
channel. Shinzen describes transverberation as Source making a wormhole or
arrow through time-space, self, and world, with void in the center of the
channel and ordinary interaction around it ([[God's Arrow]] S1-S3). This is
strong evidence for Shinzen's oral Source analogy and weak evidence for
physics, theology, or art history.

[[Shinzen's Weird Meditation]] adds the category-theory/connectivity version
of the same boundary. Shinzen marks his claim about what lies behind See Out
as a guess, then compares color/form to an abbreviated representation of a
vast network of connections and gestures toward dependent arising, entropy,
and category theory ([[Shinzen's Weird Meditation]] S6). The source is
valuable for his analogy vocabulary, not for mathematical, physical, or
ontological proof.

[[The Spring of the Void]] adds a brief free-energy and pressure analogy.
Shinzen uses a bouncing toy, Helmholtz/free-energy language, and Robert
Boyle's "spring of the air" phrase to make emptiness feel springy rather than
bleak ([[The Spring of the Void]] S1-S4). This is useful teaching idiolect,
not evidence that emptiness, consciousness, or enlightenment is explained by
physics.

[[Shinzen's Billion Dollar Hypothesis]] adds the most concrete
neurotechnology moonshot now compiled from the channel. Shinzen treats
athymhormia as a pathological clue to something like equanimity or no-self,
not as liberation; he frames virtual-lesion research by low probability,
expected value, spiritual-status assessment, and IRB-style non-harm
constraints ([[Shinzen's Billion Dollar Hypothesis]] S1-S9). This source
belongs in the research-hope tier even though the rhetoric is expansive.

[[Hacking Enlightenment Can Ultrasound Help You Transcend Reality]] adds an
external documentary frame for focused ultrasound as a possible equanimity
support. It names PCC/default-mode targeting, participant reports, and
commercialization fears, but the source is journalism and should not be used
as efficacy, safety, or clinical evidence ([[Hacking Enlightenment Can
Ultrasound Help You Transcend Reality]] S1-S9).

[[Is Fixation THE Necessary Cause for Suffering]] adds a lower-tech
measurement bridge. Shinzen treats Sasaki Roshi's fixation language as
possibly pointing toward a global nervous-system parameter, then mentions
attentional blink and craving research as possible clues. The source is
useful for Shinzen's hypothesis-space; it does not validate attentional
blink as an enlightenment metric or any intervention as practice guidance
([[Is Fixation THE Necessary Cause for Suffering]] S1-S8).

[[Fulfilling the Pythagorean Agenda]] names the larger aspiration. Shinzen
reads Pythagoras as having wanted to link outer nature, inner
thought/emotion/spirit, and number, then suggests that modern number systems,
experimental method, and concentration technology might someday model the
inner and outer "dance" mathematically. The page records an aspiration, not
an achieved model or validation ([[Fulfilling the Pythagorean Agenda]]
S1-S7).

[[Three Dances of Self and World]] gives that aspiration a chart interface.
Shinzen places the human dance of appreciate/transcend/improve, the nature
dance of Expansion-Contraction across scales, and the number dance of
complex-number polarity around Source as the still point ([[Three Dances of
Self and World]] S1-S6). This is useful evidence for how he visually
integrates the analogy set, but its Planck, Friedmann, star, complex-number,
Pythagorean, and still-point claims remain source-level teaching rhetoric,
not proof.

The time-space dialogue is lower confidence. [[Advanced Meditators Experience
of Time]] contrasts discrete prison-like relative time with continuous,
smooth, free time and then speculatively links that subjective continuum to
light, spacetime, geometry, physics, and possible future neuroscience. The
source itself is a low-quality three-speaker transcript, so photon,
manifold, Schrodinger, and measurement language should remain idiolect and
research imagination, not validated physics or a new practice primitive
([[Advanced Meditators Experience of Time]] S3).

## Boundaries

This page does not reject analogy. Analogy can help practitioners understand
polarities, remember a practice handle, or see why Shinzen cares about
contemplative science. The boundary is against proof inflation.

Cross-tradition parallels use the same rule. Jewish, Christian, Sufi, Taoist,
Zen, Native ceremony, and Sasaki Roshi idioms can be Shinzen's useful
translation bridges without proving doctrinal identity, philology,
anthropology, or one shared metaphysics. Load [[Lineage Translation]] when
authority or tradition-level claims matter.

Inherited Buddhist doctrine uses the same evidence ladder. A claim can be
important to Shinzen's teaching while still being unproven, speculative, or
only practice-testable. Reincarnation and post-mortem continuity should not
be upgraded because a science-facing talk defends other Buddhist claims.

The boundary is especially important in safety contexts. Scientific or
cosmic language should not be used to normalize destabilization, bypass
medical or clinical care, certify a teacher, or make a practitioner endure
harmful intensity.
