## Summary
Shinzen says Sounds True valued presentations that did not sound like authors reading prepared material. To create that quality for *The Science of Enlightenment*, they gathered roughly a dozen of his Colorado students in a studio for about a week while he spoke to them all day from a loose outline on cards.

He describes the result as a spontaneous spoken "brain dump" of his understanding of the meditative path, with enough live audience presence to make the recording feel addressed to real listeners. The raw recording produced about twice the final length, so Michael Taft spent months editing it down into the audio product.

The talk ends by saying Shinzen and Michael Taft were working on a text version. They initially expected transcription plus light editing, but found that the book required a radical makeover and much more time than expected.
