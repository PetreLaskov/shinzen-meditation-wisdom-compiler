## Selection Thesis

As of the 2026-05-29 policy reorientation, `@ShinzenVideos` should be treated
as a primary Shinzen teaching corpus rather than a small selective supplement.
The project should ingest most Shinzen-primary dharma, practice, wisdom,
instruction, Q&A, and transmission material from this channel when it contains
new source content or gives a reusable teaching handle, even when a stronger
owner page already exists.

The default is now inclusion. Deferral or skipping is reserved for actual
noise: non-practice/non-wisdom fragments, non-Shinzen-primary material,
manifest-only/private/silent items, promotional or event-housekeeping
material, comedy/outtakes/testimonials with no practice value, or true
duplicates where a compiled source page already covers the same video, the
same talk/session, the same transcript content, or an excerpt wholly contained
inside an already ingested source. Similar title, same topic, overlapping
theme, or a stronger existing owner page is not enough to call an item a
duplicate. Duplicate or excerpt status should be recorded as such rather than
laundered into query-driven deferral.

## Current-Model Fit

The first selected items targeted the wiki's current debt rather than the
channel's playlist order. The new channel-level objective is broader:
preserve Shinzen's practice and wisdom teaching from the channel wherever it
adds source content, oral transmission, idiolect, practice routing, safety,
service, or comparative framing.

- **Live guidance and emotional intensity**: the whole `From Suffering to
  Bliss` session gives a rare continuous example of Shinzen diagnosing,
  subdividing, zooming, and re-routing an emotional process in real time.
- **Safety and stop criteria**: the strong-determination and dark-night/void
  talks sharpen physical-damage limits, DPDR-like differentials, and integration
  routes.
- **Practice architecture**: `Organizing Your Practice` is stronger than short
  introductory clips because it treats formal practice, practice-in-life,
  cycles, triggers, and teacher contact as one scheduling problem.
- **Service and consent**: the death-process and after-enlightenment talks add
  rare applied guidance around care, consent, dying, and service.
- **Map humility**: `Pros & Cons of Dharma Maps` directly supports the wiki's
  anti-map-fixation and behavior-accountability posture; `The Agony of
  Jargon` adds the word-level map-translation caution.
