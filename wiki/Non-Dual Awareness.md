## Teaching Register
Advanced map clarification and terminology calibration. Shinzen is not mainly instructing a technique; he is reducing confusion around witness, consciousness, and non-dual awareness by assigning different phenomenological territories to a contested phrase.

## Why This Talk Matters
The talk gives Gate 6B an opening distinction: non-duality is not one experience. It may mean just seeing/hearing/touching without inner reaction, formless merging where subject and object disappear, or Shinzen's preferred stronger sense: ordinary life repeatedly born from and returning to Zero without a fundamental split between Source and daily activity.

## Key Claims
- **S1**: One possible non-dual awareness is subject-object oneness where a sight, sound, or touch arises without Feel/Image/Talk reaction, leaving just the object and no separate observer.
- **S2**: A deeper version occurs when object-side Touch/Sight/Sound and self-side Feel/Image/Talk both remain unfixated as Flow, merge as one wave, and die into Zero.
- **S3**: Shinzen prefers to reserve "non-dual awareness" for habitual abiding in the cycle of Zero, polarization, Zero, and polarization, hundreds of times a day.
- **S4**: In that preferred sense, self and world regenerate inside Zero and return quickly to Zero, so Source and ordinary daily life are not fundamentally split.
- **S5**: Shinzen links this preferred meaning to the Zen ox-herding image of being firmly mounted on the ox and unable to be bucked off by inner or outer events.

## Load-Bearing Teaching Moves
- **S1**: The talk separates non-duality from a vague doctrine by tying the simplest version to a concrete absence of Feel/Image/Talk reaction around Touch/Sight/Sound.
- **S2**: The deeper version connects no-self, Flow, Gone, and Zero: subject and object do not merely feel unified; they lose fixation, become one wave, and vanish.
- **S3-S4**: Shinzen's preferred definition shifts non-duality from a temporary merging state to a habitual daily-life rhythm where transcendent Source and ordinary functioning remain connected.
- **S5**: The ox-herding image previews later Gate 6B path-map material: stability is measured by not being thrown out of Source-emptiness participation when life becomes difficult.

## Practice Handles
- When "non-dual awareness" appears in a question, first ask which sense is intended: no inner reaction to an object, formless subject-object disappearance, or stable Source/ordinary-life nonseparation.
- For the simplest practice-facing form, watch whether Touch, Sight, or Sound is followed by Feel/Image/Talk that creates an observer over against the object.
- For the deeper impermanence form, track whether both object-side and self-side strands are unfixated Flow, then whether their merged wave dies into Gone/Zero.
- For the preferred mature form, do not treat a single formless event as the whole claim; the issue is repeated ordinary arising and return without losing the "umbilical" connection to Zero.

## Summary
Shinzen begins by warning that "witness," "consciousness," and "non-dual awareness" are used differently by different communities, which can create confusion and contention. He offers a first possible definition: a sense object arises without Feel/Image/Talk reaction, so the observer is absent and there is just sight, sound, or touch. He compares this to seed-based samadhi, because there is still an object with which merging occurs.

He then describes a deeper possible use. The object-side world and self-side reaction both become unfixated Flow, merge as one wave, and die into Zero. He compares this to seedless samadhi and to a Zen phrase meaning both subject and object have disappeared. This version transcends subject and object in a formless nothingness.

Shinzen then states his preferred use: non-dual awareness should name habitual abiding in the Zero-polarization-Zero cycle. Self and world reappear from Zero, but if their origin is recognized, they are not fundamentally severed from Source. Ordinary activity continues, but the practitioner is "born but not really born"; daily life and Source are not split. He closes with the ox-herding image of riding consciousness, emptiness, Source, God, or nature without being bucked off by inner or outer conditions.

## Source Frame
