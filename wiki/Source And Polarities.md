Source and Polarities is the first-load hub for Shinzen's deepest polarity
language. It keeps Zero, Gone, Expansion-Contraction, Source afterglow,
science analogy, and shared-Source service in one routable frame without
turning any of them into established metaphysics, physics, or conduct proof.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Shinzen's Source language is a contemplative polarity model:
  sensory events arise through Expansion and Contraction, return through Gone,
  and are interpreted as Zero or Source only under strict afterglow limits.
- **Why this matters**: The page prevents advanced Flow, Gone, Do Nothing,
  bhanga, nondual, time-space, and service language from becoming either vague
  mysticism or overconfident science.
- **Key tensions**: Practice-level force labels are stronger than claims about
  objective reality; service language is stronger as aspiration and kinship
  rationale than as evidence of competence or ethical reliability.
- **Source posture**: Strong as Shinzen's own teaching frame; probable as a
  practice synthesis; speculative for objective reality, science, cosmology,
  mathematics, theology, or spontaneous service guarantees.

## Hub Model

The local practice root is [[Expansion And Contraction]]. In the manual's Flow
teaching, Expansion means spreading, increasing, or scattering; Contraction
means squeezing, decreasing, or gripping; and Both means simultaneous
Expansion-Contraction. At the deepest level, Shinzen says each moment can be
read as Nothingness polarizing into both forces and returning to Nothingness
when the forces cancel ([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 4 - The Way of Flow]]
S6-S7).

[[Gone]] is the main Source-facing doorway. Science of Enlightenment chapter 9
says ordinary Gones point toward the Uncreated or Source while also clarifying
that Source is not itself a sensory experience; the sensory side is the
aftereffect of vanishing becoming clearer ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter
9 - The Power of Gone]] S9). Chapter 10 then makes the polarity model more
explicit: each sensory or motor event is born from simultaneous
Expansion-Contraction, and Gone is the mutual cancellation of those polar
forces into Zero ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the
Source]] S2-S4, S12-S13).

The governing calibration is the afterglow boundary. In the cancellation
itself there is no self present to know Source directly; what appears next is
a conditioned representation made of image, talk, feeling, fulfillment,
safety, peace, and love ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to
the Source]] S14-S15). Use [[Source Afterglow Boundary]] whenever a claim
risks treating Source, Zero, or contentless consciousness as a directly
perceived object.

The frame also functions as translation language. Shinzen maps Jewish,
Christian, Sufi, Taoist, Zen, Native ceremony, Sasaki Roshi, ox-herding,
shunya/kenosis, and true-love idioms into Source, Zero, Flow, Gone, and
Expansion-Contraction. Those pages are evidence for Shinzen's teaching
bridges, not independent proof that the traditions share one metaphysics.
When cross-tradition authority matters, route through [[Lineage Translation]]
and the relevant source page.

[[Shinzen Young Retreat at the Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]] adds a long
retreat implementation of the Source-polarity route. Shinzen first warms up
ordinary Just See, Just Hear, Just Feel, and Note Everything, then asks
practitioners to detect starts, stops, simultaneous start-stop, and rest as
yes, no, both, and rest across visual, auditory, somatic, and all-sensory
domains ([[Shinzen Young Retreat at the Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]]
S3-S10). This confirms that Source language is operationally introduced
through sensory contour and equanimity cues, not by asking practitioners to
believe a metaphysical proposition first.

[[Visual Presentation of Mindfulness and the Spiritual Path Part 2]] gives a
board-chart version of the same deep/broad polarity frame. Shinzen places
the practice-level interplay of parts/whole, distinctions/unifications,
active/passive, and self/no-self beside sensory Expansion-Contraction and a
broad contrast-and-cancellation analogy across nature and mathematics (S4-S5).
The source is useful for transmission, but its cosmology and mathematics
claims belong under the science-analogy boundary.

[[Touching the Heart]] gives the absolute-now version of this polarity
language. Shinzen distinguishes relative present-centeredness from absolute
present-centeredness, then describes the absolute now as continuous arisings
spreading into space, passings collapsing space, and surface self/world
experience born like foam between Expansion and Contraction ([[Touching the
Heart]] S3-S5). It is useful as an oral Source-polarity anchor, but its
direct-contact and eternal-identity phrasing still needs the afterglow and
science-analogy boundaries ([[Touching the Heart]] S6-S7).

[[Three Watershed Moments]] adds an autobiographical micro-anchor for the
same polarity vocabulary. Shinzen says he vaguely remembers an early state of
expanding-contracting effortless space before objectified self and world, then
later interprets worry, thought, and fear as the inner system arising as a
self. This supports Source-polarity as his teaching frame while requiring
strong memory, developmental, and metaphysical calibration ([[Three Watershed
Moments]] S1-S5).

[[Shinzen's Weird Meditation]] adds an advanced visual unfixation anchor. In a
personal See Out/See Rest episode, Shinzen says outer color/form became vivid
and paper-thin before ordinary objectification, while inner and outer visual
worlds later seemed to merge into every church, synagogue, mosque, and related
sacred space. The source supports Source-polarity and connectivity rhetoric
only under strict non-imitation, altered-phenomena, and science-analogy
boundaries ([[Shinzen's Weird Meditation]] S1-S6).

[[God's Arrow]] adds the transverberation branch of Shinzen's Source rhetoric.
He uses St. Teresa/Bernini, a Source wormhole through time-space, and void in
the center of a channel while ordinary color, form, and interaction remain
around it ([[God's Arrow]] S1-S3). The practical boundary is unusually clear:
when Source is not salient during the day, mature functioning may look like
ordinary agitated business, but without preference for deep state over task
activity ([[God's Arrow]] S4-S5). This strengthens Source-polarity as oral
transmission while keeping the wormhole and no-perceptible-breath language
under afterglow, science-analogy, and safety boundaries.

[[The Spring of the Void]] adds the compact positive-void idiom. Shinzen says
vastness is only half the image of emptiness, bleakness is a common
misreading, and his own meditative image is bounciness or springiness: directly
touching the spring of the void (S1-S2). This supports the polarity hub as a
living Source register, while the toy, Helmholtz/free-energy, and Boyle
pressure analogy remain bounded as source-level metaphor ([[The Spring of the
Void]] S3-S4).

[[Three Dances of Self and World]] adds a visual integration anchor. Shinzen
places three dances around the still point of Source: the human dance of the
three jobs and CCE skills, the nature dance of Expansion-Contraction across
scales, and the number dance of complex-number polarity ([[Three Dances of
Self and World]] S1-S6). This strengthens Source-polarity as a chart idiom,
but the nature, mathematics, Pythagorean, and literary material belongs under
[[Source Science And Analogy Boundary]].

The Great Dharma world-mysticism pair adds two clean oral Source-polarity
anchors. [[Journey to True Spirituality]] describes self/world as an
effortless efflux and reflux, a formless fountain whose dynamic side is
Expansion-Contraction and whose still side is absolute rest; the service
boundary is that Source contact should contextualize ordinary judgment rather
than replace discernment ([[Journey to True Spirituality]] S9-S10). [[World
Mysticism with a Capital M]] gives the form/emptiness version: form is
emptiness routes through Flow and Gone, while emptiness is form routes through
Zero breaking into affirmation and negation, Expansion and Contraction, and
momentary self/world manifestation ([[World Mysticism with a Capital M]]
S7-S10).

Science, mathematics, nature, and time-space parallels are lower-confidence
extensions. Shinzen explicitly agrees that mystical experience does not
settle objective reality claims, and his free-energy, entropy,
thermodynamics, dialectics, complex-number, nature, and time-space language
should stay analogical or speculative unless a source-level question asks for
the exact rhetoric ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the
Source]] S6; [[Reality & Sensory Experience]] S1-S5). Use [[Source Science
And Analogy Boundary]] for those claims. The 2017 Monastic Academy stream
adds free-energy and evoked-potential language to this same evidence tier:
it is useful for Shinzen's teaching imagination, not proof that Source or
Expansion-Contraction has been established scientifically ([[Shinzen Young
Retreat at the Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]] S8).

Service is the return-to-life side of the same frame. Chapter 10 says shared
Source realization can make others appear as close kin and call the
practitioner toward spontaneous service ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter
10 - Return to the Source]] S20). That is a strong Shinzen-internal
interpretation, but it does not verify behavior, consent, role competence,
repair, or teaching readiness. Use [[Source And Service Boundary]] before
treating Source language as service authority.

## Safety And Boundaries

Source language can destabilize or overinflate practice if loaded too early.
Gone, emptiness, no-self, bhanga, and Source-side spaciousness can involve
fear, loss, disorientation, comparison, craving, or unsupported collapse.
When distress, DPDR-like flattening, void fear, practice worsening, or
behavior mismatch appears, load [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] and
its child pages before optimizing the Source interpretation.

The page should not replace concrete technique owners. Load [[Flow]] for
movement/change/energy practice, [[Gone]] for vanishing practice,
[[Expansion And Contraction]] for force-pattern practice, [[Surface To
Source]] for the compact ordinary-to-Source route, and [[Impermanence Flow
Gone And Source]] for the whole impermanence progression.

It should also not become a prestige map. Shinzen's own sources repeatedly
make the vocabulary optional, comparative, poetic, or speculative in places.
A practitioner can route through CCE, Noting, Do Nothing, Flow, Gone, service,
or ordinary repair without adopting Source/polarity cosmology.

## Implications

When future sources mention Source, Nothingness, Zero, polarity, or cosmology,
classify the claim before using it:

- practice instruction,
- phenomenological report,
- symbolic mnemonic,
- comparative translation,
- analogy,
- metaphysical claim,
- scientific speculation,
- service rationale,
- or safety-relevant state.

That classification keeps the deep frame usable while preserving confidence
boundaries.
