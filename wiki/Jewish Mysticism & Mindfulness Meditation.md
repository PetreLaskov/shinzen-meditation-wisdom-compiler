## Why This Talk Matters
The talk shows a clean instance of Shinzen's translation method. He begins
with the operational body-mind grammar of touch, feel, image, and talk, then
adds rates of change as Flow, vanishing as Gone, and a One-Zero Source that
links individual body-mind process to all of creation.

## Key Claims
- **S1**: Shinzen defines the broad self as body-mind experience and maps it to touch, feel, image, and talk.
- **S2**: Close attention to those sensory categories reveals rates of change and underlying impersonal doing pervading body physicality, body emotionality, and mental processes.
- **S3**: He links this impersonal doing to Rinzai's "true/authentic person" with no fixed position and to the metaphor of an invisible hand inside a puppet.
- **S4**: He says Kabbalistic masters use a similar invisible-hand metaphor and interprets the expanding, contracting, vibrating field as churning body-mind self into existence from a cleft or womb-like nothing.
- **S5**: He presents nondual awareness as continuity between nothing, nothing polarizing, physical-emotional-mental self arising, and reabsorption back into energy that flatlines again.
- **S6**: He says the advanced practice mark is continuity of this Zero-polarization-arising-reabsorption process during ordinary world activity, which he calls the beginning of Zen-master-class practice.
- **S7**: He maps the Kabbalistic person/soul layers as physical body, emotional body, mental process, impersonal energy corresponding to Flow or qi, and a One/Source corresponding to Gone or vanishing.
- **S8**: He says all zeros are the same Zero, so anchoring in Gone/Zero gives a felt overlap of one's origin with the origin of all creation and a sense of fraternity and sorority with everything.

## Load-Bearing Teaching Moves
- **S1-S2**: Shinzen uses his own primitive sensory grammar first. The comparative frame is mounted on touch/feel/image/talk and Flow, not the other way around.
- **S4-S5**: The Kabbalistic bridge translates a body-mind arising cycle into the same Source-polarity model already used for Sasaki, Cohen, and Gone practice.
- **S6**: The daily-life criterion prevents the map from becoming private metaphysical fascination; continuity while "taking care of business" is the practice maturity test.
- **S7-S8**: The person/soul mapping extends Gate 8 with a Jewish mystical route into shared Source kinship, but it depends on Shinzen's oral translation and should remain source-attributed.

## Practice Handles
- Start with the primitive sensory layer: touch, feel, image, and talk.
- When change becomes salient, include Flow as the impersonal doing that pervades body, emotion, and mind.
- When vanishing becomes salient, note Gone as the return-to-Zero side of the same process.
- Test maturity by whether Zero-arising-return continuity can be known during ordinary activity, not only in seated or conceptual contemplation.
- Use shared-Zero kinship as a service and humility implication, not as proof that one has mastered Kabbalah, Zen, or metaphysics.

## Summary
Shinzen opens by saying that a Lurianic or Kabbalistic concept of human being
or soul maps closely to how he presents meditation techniques. Broadly
defined, the self is body-mind experience: touch, feel, image, and talk.
Attending closely to these categories reveals rates of change and, beneath
them, impersonal doing that pervades body, emotion, and mind.

He then connects that impersonal doing to Zen and Kabbalistic metaphors. The
true person has no fixed position and is better understood as doing than as a
thing; the invisible hand inside the puppet becomes a cross-tradition image
for the force churning body-mind self into existence. The field expands,
contracts, vibrates, wells up, subsides, and returns to a One-Zero nothing.
For Shinzen, nondual awareness here means continuity between nothing,
polarization, self/world arising, and reabsorption.

The closing mapping is the most practice-relevant part. Shinzen associates
physicality, emotionality, and mental process with touch, feel, image, and
talk; the impersonal energy layer with Flow or qi; and the One/Source layer
with Gone or vanishing. Because all Gones or zeros are "the same zero," he
says one's origin overlaps with the origin of every other piece of creation,
giving a felt kinship with all of creation.
