## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Gate 4 makes impermanence a route, not a topic: ordinary change can be clarified as Flow, abrupt diminishment as Gone, stable pressure as simultaneous Expansion-Contraction, and Source as the afterglow boundary around cancellation.
- **Why this matters**: Future agents can explain advanced Source language from manageable sensory handles instead of jumping from metaphysics to instruction.
- **Key tensions**: The same sources use bold life/death, nirvana, Christian, Zen, mathematical, and future-science language; all of that remains source-attributed and safety-bounded.
- **Source posture**: Probable as a synthesis of Shinzen's compiled Gate 4 talks; speculative when extended to physics, mathematics, neuroscience, theology, clinical diagnosis, or verified service outcomes.

## Landscape
- **Ordinary change becomes practice** ([[Impermanence]], [[Flow]]) - Gate 4 keeps Flow demystified: change includes ordinary pulse, increase/decrease, pressure, scattering, gripping, vibration, and force when these are actually detectable rather than manufactured ([[The Focus on Flow Theme]] S1-S4; [[The Theme of Expansive and Contractive Flow]] S1-S5).
- **Vanishings stay ordinary** ([[Gone]]) - Gone need not be grand cessation; breath endings, mental-talk endings, partial drops, and non-permanent abrupt diminishings are sufficient practice objects ([[Abrupt Flow Diminishings, Vanishings and Noting Gone]] S1-S4).
- **Expansion-Contraction matures the change frame** ([[Expansion And Contraction]]) - passing-as-arising, breath volume versus force, three-dimensional spherical imagery, and observer-including participation all refine arising-and-passing beyond a watcher noting events from outside ([[Born Between Expansion and Contraction]] S1-S3; [[Paradigms of Change]] S5-S6; [[Mindfulness Momentum, Arising and Passing to Simultaneous Expansion and Contraction]] S5-S7).
- **Flow and Gone can reverse figure and ground** ([[Flow]], [[Gone]]) - modality, object, and self/world specificity may naturally drop out until Flow-Gone rhythm becomes the ground from which self and world appear ([[Flow, Gone and a Figure-Ground Reversal]] S1-S5).
- **Surface routes to Source through sensory completion** ([[Surface To Source]], [[Source And Polarities]]) - ordinary or restful untangling can lead through Flow and Gone toward Source language, while the afterglow boundary prevents Source from becoming another sensory object ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S1-S7; [[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S14-S15).
- **Dissolution is optional and safety-relevant** ([[Dissolution]]) - bhanga may be absent, blissful, harsh, or mixed; heavenly and hellish versions both need support and differential caution rather than status comparison ([[Dissolution (Bhanga), and T.S. Eliot]] S2-S5; [[Experiences of the Dissolution (Bhanga) Process - 2 of 3]] S1-S4).
- **Service is the maturity test** ([[Total Happiness]]) - Source contact is not self-validating; the Gate 4 gold standard is mutual reinforcement between getting over self/world and improving self/world ([[From Surface to Source and the Gold Standard for Spiritual Maturity]] S6-S7).

## Through-Line
Gate 4's through-line is sensory continuity from ordinary perception to advanced Source language. The sequence does not ask a practitioner to believe in Source first. It starts with present sensory events: an itch, breath, pulse, talk burst, field of Rest, pressure, image, sound, or emotional body sensation can be clarified as changing, flowing, vanishing, stretching, squeezing, or resting.

The decisive refinement is that impermanence becomes volumetric and participatory. Earlier arising-and-passing can imply an observer watching change. Gate 4 repeatedly pressures that stance: the apparent observer is itself Feel/Image/Talk, the apparent world is Touch/Sight/Sound, and both can be felt as shaped inside the same Expansion-Contraction field ([[Paradigms of Change]] S5-S6; [[Mindfulness Momentum, Arising and Passing to Simultaneous Expansion and Contraction]] S5-S7).

This explains why [[Expansion And Contraction]] now deserves mature owner-page status rather than a further split in this pass. It is doing two linked jobs: a practical Flow taxonomy for force patterns, and the bridge into [[Source And Polarities]]. A future split may be warranted if the science, mathematics, and comparative-religion material swells; for now the page's boundaries keep those extensions calibrated.

Gate 4 also clarifies the route through [[Gone]]. Gone is not merely the opposite of Flow. In practice, it is the noticed abrupt diminishing or vanishing. In the deeper model, repeated Gones and Flow-Gone rhythms can make vanishing or impermanence itself become the ground from which self and world arise. That shift remains practice-relevant only when it preserves clarity and does not become dissociation or void fixation.

[[Surface To Source]] is the best compact route created by the gate. It keeps Source tied to ordinary/restful untangling, Flow, Gone, afterglow, and improvement/service accountability. This prevents Source language from floating free as metaphysics and gives future agents a mid-level explanation between basic CCE and deep polarity claims.
