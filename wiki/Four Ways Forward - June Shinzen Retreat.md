## Why This Talk Matters

## Summary
Shinzen opens with broad Note Everything: know whether attention is on See,
Hear, or Feel, use labels as needed, and let detail remain optional. The
first long sit then shows how broad practice can mature or strain: detail,
integrated unity, flooding, or decentering may appear. The practical fixes
come first--narrow, re-note, or zoom--while the advanced reading treats
scattering and decentering as Expansion-Contraction when capacity allows.

The middle trains Auto Move and Feel Good. Auto Move turns walking into
motor-output practice by detecting movement happening by itself. Feel Good
deliberately cultivates positive body emotion and treats lovingkindness as
mindfulness when positive Feel is held, clarified, and allowed while
nonpositive material remains in the background. The Q&A keeps the method
menu light: switch by interest, opportunity, or necessity, not by craving,
aversion, or unconsciousness.

The closing block explores Space and polarity by Zooming Beyond body,
auditory, and visual boundaries into openness while noticing dropouts and
endings as Gone or contraction. Shinzen reviews the day as appreciation,
transcendence/release, expression, and positivity, each serving CCE and the
five life goals, then returns to ordinary safety: driving or heavy machinery
permits only sensory driving practice.
