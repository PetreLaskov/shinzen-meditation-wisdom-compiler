## Practice Handles
When broad pleasant Flow is already present, begin with spoken "Flow" labels to stabilize contact, then allow mental labels or no labels if they work better. Each note should include a zoom-out option: cover as much of body, mind, and outside world as the Flow actually occupies.

If sight is involved, test with soft eyes-open looking rather than forcing visual effects. If the visual world begins to present more as light than solid sight, include that light as part of the Flow field.

For advanced students with stable equanimity and consent, a guide may test ordinary-life transfer by adding eye contact. The point is not social performance; it is whether Flow remains available when relational contact increases the challenge.
