# Index

This wiki tracks Shinzen Young's meditation practice and wisdom system as a
compiled working model, not as a transcript archive or general Buddhism
encyclopedia. Route first by practice handle, phenomenological distinction,
technique choice, teaching move, transformation mechanism, safety boundary,
and service implication.

Current coverage includes the full Basic Mindfulness manual, *The Science of
Enlightenment* chapters 1-11, [[What Is Mindfulness]], the eight-unit See Hear
Feel introduction, and the YouTube plan through Gate 7 item 9. Detailed gate
chronology belongs in `wiki/log.md` and
`wiki/_yt_ingestion_implementation_plan.md`; this index should stay a skim
surface.

is best modeled as sensory completion with interface evolution. Use [[Basic

The wiki is at a first-pass post-book-plus-SHF-plus-YouTube-Gate-7-item-9
stage with 212 compiled pages. Gate 4 is checkpointed: ordinary change routes through
Flow and Gone; pressure and apparent solidity can be parsed as simultaneous
Expansion-Contraction; Source remains bounded as afterglow rather than a
direct sensory object; bhanga and void-side destabilization route through
safety pages; and service/improvement of self and world is the maturity test.
Gate 5 has begun by grounding the sensory grammar in Shinzen's
six-consciousnesses translation, contrast-based Basic States design, and
dynamic-aspect overlay for Flow and Gone inside ordinary/restful categories,
then completing the Discrimination and Unification series as a dialectic:
distinguish components, digest them into Flow, refine Flow into polarity and
spacious self/world relation when available, then keep returning to healthy
distinctions. The Depth and Breadth of Concentration series now frames
concentration as intrinsically rewarding, trainable in both depth and
breadth, relevance-based rather than necessarily narrow, and vulnerable to
aversive suppression when non-target material is fought, while completing
the series by distinguishing initial effort, possible automaticity, renewed
effort under sensory challenge, and modern upaya instead of brute-force
ordeal. Gate 5 item 11 now adds a growth-and-taste model for the whole CCE
triad: local critical mass, uneven long-term growth, concentration's in-zone
reward, equanimity's release taste, and sensory clarity as unmixing,
resolution, subtle detection, and speed. Gate 5 items 12-13 sharpen no-self
as direct Feel/Image/Talk sensory decomposition, then add a CRT-style
resolution metaphor and a discrimination-to-vibrating-Flow follow-through,
while Gate 5 item 14 adds an ordinary/restful monitoring experiment: reactive
Feel/Image/Talk can build the perceiving self, inactive or untangled
Feel/Image/Talk can reveal no-self, and six simultaneous Rest events can keep
tranquility insight-bearing. Gate 5 item 15 names sensory clarity as
experiential inward analysis: break limited identity into components, see the
interactions, and gain a handle on suffering without making any one inherited
taxonomy primary. Gate 5 item 16 makes that taxonomy flexibility explicit:
the six sensory spaces can be grouped as body/mind/world,
subjective/objective, or somatic/visual/auditory, and the right grouping is
the one that works under practice criteria. Gate 5 item 17 adds a concrete
See Flow handle: detect gross, eye-level, and small attentional shifts in
sight space as new visual instances. Gate 5 item 18 completes the queued Gate
5 source sequence by distinguishing body/self image, ambient place image, and
central memory-plan-fantasy imagery as image-space locations. Gate 6A item 1
opens the operational-enlightenment sequence by defining liberation as
identity no longer being trapped by thoughts and body sensations while keeping
mind-body as a home rather than a prison. Gate 6A item 2 adds map humility:
no current enlightenment map, teacher, or tradition should be treated as
final, and future science/practice technology may improve access while
remaining speculative and safety-sensitive. Gate 6A item 3 adds the
post-realization integration rule: enlightenment is not a clean before/after
binary, sudden no-self still leaves behavior work and outside support needs,
and love is named as the point after meaning-demand loosens. Gate 6A item 4
adds the expectation-calibration rule: enlightenment may be profoundly
valuable, but it is not permanent bliss, personal perfection, easy
communicability, or self-owned influence. Gate 6A item 5 adds the good-place
trap rule: maps, closed belief systems, tranquility, powers, enlightenment,
and observer identity can each help practice but stall it when they replace
practice, clarity, deconstruction, feedback, or automatic CCE. Gate 6A item
6 adds the scale-and-conduct boundary: mass stream entry may heal the world
over long time, but real no-self still permits ordinary human screw-ups. Gate
6A item 7 adds the DPDR differential: no-self and paper-thin world can be
liberating in enlightenment or disabling in depersonalization-derealization
distress, so void-side experience must be routed by valence, function,
support, and reconstruction capacity. Gate 6A item 8 adds that apparent dark-night or pit-of-the-void
distress usually may include ordinary depression, anxiety, or clinical
factors, so professional assessment and medication-scope humility are part of
the safety boundary. Gate 6A item 9 adds the compact advanced-student
emptiness regimen: empty the fear or disorientation itself, then intentionally
rebuild a positive conditioned self through sustained positive Feel/Image/Talk.
Gate 6A item 10 adds the teacher-qualification boundary: liberation signs and
no-self-like body language do not qualify a teacher without ordinary behavior
and student-results checks. Gate 6A item 11 adds feedback-loop ethics:
teacher blind spots persist when pedestal dynamics, independence, or
spiritual dismissal block correction, so ethics requires open channels for
human feedback and consensus patterns over time. Gate 6A item 12 completes
the current operational-enlightenment gate by adding the anti-dependency
rule: competent liberation teaching should transfer principles, skills, and
confidence so students become independent peers rather than dependent
teacher-followers. Gate 6B item 1 opens the no-self/witness/path-map sequence
by distinguishing three meanings of non-dual awareness: object contact without
Feel/Image/Talk observer reaction, formless subject-object disappearance, and
habitual Zero-polarization-Zero daily-life cycling. Gate 6B item 2 adds the
self-enquiry bridge: self-enquiry looks back toward the origin of an arising,
while Gone watches a passing into the same Source-facing boundary.
Gate 6B item 3 adds the witness router: the detached relative witness is a
useful equanimity metaphor produced by subtle Feel/Image/Talk, while the
absolute witness is contentless cessation or Zero reached where observer-
making Feel/Image/Talk disappears.
Gate 6B item 4 adds the selflessness progression: Feel/Image/Talk can be
untangled, known as waves, stop into Zero, and re-arise as healthy
personality rather than a coagulated ego-problem.
Gate 6B item 5 adds the comparative humility frame: no-self is read across
traditions as vanishing self/world somethingness, but the mature pattern
returns as Source stabilization, doingness, goodness, compassion, and
service rather than blank annihilation.
Gate 6B item 6 adds the no-place-to-stand router: breath home bases and
riverbank equanimity metaphors are valid supports, but Shinzen sometimes
withholds a fixed center so decentering and figure-ground reversal can occur
when concentration, clarity, and equanimity are adequate.
Gate 6B item 7 adds the ox-herding path-map router: searching is
conditional-happiness seeking, footprints and partial ox-sighting are
inspiration and glimpse, catching the ox is first no-self/stream-entry/kensho,
riding the ox backwards is surrender to impermanence, and the last three
pictures are no substance, ordinary appearance, and marketplace service.
Gate 6B item 8 confirms the opening ox-herding sequence in the three-part
series and sharpens the diagnostic difference between searching, traces,
partial vipassana glimpse, and first no-self/kensho capture.
Gate 6B item 9 adds the middle and late ox-herding sequence in the three-part
series: taming stabilizes no-self, riding the ox backward surrenders
orientation to impermanence, homecoming is repose, and the final triad begins
with no substance and ordinary appearance.
Gate 6B item 10 completes the ox-herding sequence: marketplace service is
the final cause of practice, expressed as ordinary approachable presence,
practice effects that teach through the body, coherent description when
asked, and eventual teaching for some people when students appear.
Gate 7 item 1 opens the Total Happiness series by treating happiness as a
technical universal aim, then reducing condition-dependent happiness to
trackable sensory experience without dismissing objective conditions.
Gate 7 item 2 adds the ordinary/extraordinary happiness contrast:
body-side happiness shifts from pleasure and discomfort management to
fulfillment and freedom from suffering, while mind-side happiness shifts
from ordinary answers and Don't Know avoidance to "what am I?" and
Source-knowing. Gate 7 item 3 adds Don't Know Mind as the mind-side practice
bridge: disciplined suspension of answer-hunger and equanimity with confusion
can become a new kind of knowing, while senility/Alzheimer's examples require
medical and safety calibration. Gate 7 item 4 adds the behavior and
self/other accountability branch: extraordinary happiness includes
character, ethics, best-effort performance, and outside-support humility, and
Total Happiness remains incomplete without ordinary service to others and
Source-based concern for others' happiness. Gate 7 item 5 completes the
series by defining teaching as fostering others' extraordinary happiness
through subtle presence, coherent description, explicit instruction,
enlightenment-edge competence, rare path discovery, and support for teachers.
Gate 7 item 6 adds bodhicitta as an empty-service polarity: compassion,
service, and love stand alongside wisdom, while the world is to be saved
without being reified and the human self returns from nothingness as kinder
and service-capable. Gate 7 item 7 adds a final-stage service bridge:
ordinary-life self/world/Source nonseparation should be actual sensory
experience rather than philosophy, and shared Zero links going beyond the
world with serving one's larger identity. Gate 7 item 8 adds a Human
Goodness service bridge: lovingkindness is Focus on Positive Feel, and
advanced positive Feel can radiate beyond the body as a high-wattage
broadcast of human positivity while remaining an anecdotal interpersonal
claim.
Gate 7 item 9 adds an empowering-contrasts maturity rule: Shinzen's path
arises between complementary sides, so discrimination and unification,
self/world deconstruction and reconstruction, bearing down and easing up, and
labeling and dropping labels are worked by timing rather than treated as
contradictions.

## Domain: Practice Architecture
Reusable models for how Shinzen's methods train skills, move through daily
life, and route practitioner attention.

- [[Basic Mindfulness Practice Architecture]] - Basic Mindfulness is a modular
  CCE-based system using the Five Ways, full Sensory Grid, expanded focus
  options, Gone, three techniques, session setup modes, life cycles, guidance,
  and total happiness. (importance 10, working, probable)
- [[See Hear Feel]] - the newer Unified Mindfulness noting interface compresses
  the older grid through flexible See/Hear/Feel labels, focus ranges, Noting
  options, practice rhythm, and old/new compatibility. (importance 10,
  working, probable)
- [[Practice Entry and Method Choice]] - Gate 1 begins method choice from entry
  aims, CCE assumptions, reaction/support needs, pragmatic path fit,
  attention-coverage and formal-session setup strategy, and Noting-option
  tuning, including forced labels as first gear, effort cross-testing,
  clarity-momentum checks for broad awareness, sole-method Do Nothing
  safeguards for attention difficulty, and Rest/Do Nothing clarification for
  nothing-is-happening reports, rather than absolute path superiority.
  (importance 9, working, probable)
- [[Self-Inquiry]] - Shinzen treats self-inquiry as a powerful but
  fit-dependent way to turn awareness back toward its source, symmetrical
  with Gone because self-inquiry follows arisings back while Gone follows
  passings back, and now bounded by the witness distinction: a detached
  watcher is useful equanimity, not the absolute witness. (importance 8,
  working, probable)
- [[Strong Determination]] - voluntary immobility is a high-intensity
  touch-plus-Feel/Image/Talk route whose value depends on sensory learning,
  non-fighting, no bodily harm, and CCE rather than duration, pain level,
  heroic endurance, or personal-record striving.
  (importance 8, working, probable)
- [[Turn Toward and Turn Away]] - Gate 2 routes live challenges through direct
  contact, background equanimity, Flow, Rest, positive Feel, or ordinary
  support, while preserving the boundary between valid focus-away and
  avoidance, suppression, dissociation, or neglect. (importance 9, working,
  probable)
- [[Effort Regulation]] - effort regulation means training freedom across
  bearing down and easing up: systematic methods can apply effort, Do Nothing
