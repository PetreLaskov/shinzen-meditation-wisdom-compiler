## Summary
Shinzen says a wisdom voice can be understood as wisdom activity rising into communication through Image or Talk. In this short clip the relevant mode is Talk. He compares it to spontaneous action: a person may move without thinking, and likewise a thought can happen without the usual deliberative sense of doing the thinking.

He then says this voice may eventually come through speech and that cultures have different wisdom-voice styles. He names a lofty style, a down-to-earth or homey Zen style, and a quirky humorous Jewish style. Because the transcript is clipped and noisy, the wiki should preserve the practice-facing distinction while avoiding strong claims about prajna, Zen, Jewish wisdom, or channeling.
