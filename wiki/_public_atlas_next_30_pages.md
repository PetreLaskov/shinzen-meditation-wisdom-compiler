## Selection Rules

- Rely on compiled `wiki/` pages first; do not reopen raw sources unless a
  public claim needs source-level checking.
- Add pages that improve public routing, safety, practice discrimination, or
  source calibration.
- Do not duplicate the existing 35 pages. A new page should own a sharper
  public job than the page it links from.
- Treat high-risk territory as publishable only after the boundary page is
  explicit: clinical scope, teacher authority, dissociation, intensity,
  altered phenomena, Source claims, consent, behavior, and ordinary support.
- Keep the atlas in the editorial middle: richer than a brochure, lighter
  than the internal agent-memory wiki.

## Page Writing Contract

Each new page should usually keep the established public shape:

1. Plain opening claim
2. Why this matters
3. Core distinction
4. How it shows up in practice
5. Common confusions
6. Safety and scope
7. Source posture
8. Next reading

Before drafting each page, load the named compiled owner page and only its
nearest dependencies. After drafting, update `public-atlas/index.md`,
`public-atlas/glossary.md` when terms are added, and relevant "Next Reading"
links. Run the atlas validation checks from `public-atlas/README.md`; if
internal `wiki/` files change, run `tools\wiki_lint.cmd`.
