## Practice Handles
- If local physical discomfort has become Flow, search the broader body for related Flow rather than staying only with the original hot spot.
- Use a body inventory for tingling, waving, pressure, expansion, and contraction when Flow may have become global.
- Zoom out when the Flow field is widespread enough that whole-body awareness supports contact better than local tracking.
- Test daily-life transfer by opening the eyes with soft focus and seeing whether some Flow remains available.
- Shift domains deliberately: after body Flow stabilizes, inspect the closed-eye visual blank for subtle movement, flicker, or changing brightness.
- Do not manufacture Flow in Rest. If the visual blank or mental image is stable, stay with stability and let it be stable.
