# Public Atlas Load-Bearing Delta Review - 2026-05-31

This is an internal editorial review note, not evidence for Shinzen claims.
It asks what the public atlas still lacks from the compiler layer and which
delta should cross into `public-atlas/` next.

## Review Scope

Current public state:

- `public-atlas/` has 65 public content pages plus README.
- The original next-30 expansion queue is complete.
- The first spine, route-test, technique/boundary source-trail, transformation
  source-trail, glossary, mixed-method, and Source/science route passes are
  complete.

Compiler surfaces inspected:

- `wiki/index.md` first-load routing and open questions.
- Recent `wiki/log.md` atlas entries.
- `wiki/_page_catalog.md` high-importance non-source pages.
- `wiki/_public_atlas_next_30_pages.md` deferred-but-important section.
- Public atlas spine and handoff pages.
- Target internal pages for the strongest candidate gaps.

## Main Judgment

The atlas is no longer missing its basic spine. The load-bearing question is
now: which internal distinctions would change public reader judgment enough
to justify adding or expanding pages?

The strongest next delta is:

> Promote Shinzen's Total Happiness aim taxonomy into the public atlas as a
> clearer page or major expansion: three jobs, four quadrants, five
> applications, ordinary/extraordinary happiness, and the behavior/service
> test.

This is more load-bearing than another generic polish pass because it changes
how the whole atlas answers "what is practice for?" The current public
`total-happiness.md` preserves the four quadrants, but it does not yet expose
the full internal aim structure that the compiler now treats as central.

## Ranked Missing Or Under-Carried Deltas

### 1. Total Happiness Aim Structure

Internal owner: [[Total Happiness Aim Structure]]

Current atlas coverage:

- `total-happiness.md` has surface/deep happiness for self/others.
- `behavior-and-service-test.md` tests practice claims by conduct and service.
- `source-service-and-bodhicitta.md` connects Source language to service.

Missing public delta:

- The three jobs: appreciate self/world, transcend self/world, improve
  self/world.
- The five applications: reduce suffering, elevate fulfillment, understand
  oneself, change behavior, and cultivate or discover love/service.
- The ordinary/extraordinary happiness distinction.
- The periodic-table-of-happiness teaching move, with claim-tier caution.
- The practical reciprocity: improve self/world can support transcendence,
  not only follow it.

Why it is load-bearing:

- It is importance 9 internally and linked from the importance-10 Total
  Happiness hub.
- Recent source work strengthened it through [[Appreciate Transcend Improve
  Our Three Human Jobs]], [[Chart of Human Happiness]], and [[Changing the
  Course of Human History And Happiness]].
- It gives the public atlas a better answer to the investor/product question:
  this is not only a technique map; it is an aim architecture.
- It improves routing for service, ethics, behavior change, constructive
  practice, public mindfulness, and Source/service boundaries without adding
  risky advanced phenomenology first.

Recommended public move:

- Create `public-atlas/total-happiness-aim-structure.md` or expand
  `public-atlas/total-happiness.md` substantially.
- Prefer a new page if the public table becomes more than a short section.
- Link from `total-happiness.md`, `behavior-and-service-test.md`,
  `source-service-and-bodhicitta.md`, `what-shinzens-system-is.md`,
  `index.md`, and `glossary.md`.

Suggested public title:

- `Total Happiness Aim Structure`
- Alternate more human title: `Three Jobs of Practice`

### 2. Auto Output Practice Family

Internal owners: [[Auto Output Practice Family]], [[Auto Move]]

Current atlas coverage:

- Nearly absent from public content.
- Spontaneous movement is named only generically inside intensity safety.

Missing public delta:

- Shinzen's Auto family is not just movement. It includes body movement, speech
  or chant, thought, and combined output practice.
- The practice target is detected self-organization under CCE, not impulse,
  unconscious drift, revelation, or conduct authority.
- The safety boundary is central: task safety, consent, vocal strain, social
  exposure, truth-checking, feedback, and stopping ability.

Why it is load-bearing:

- It is one of the few importance-8 internal concepts with almost no public
  representation.
- It makes no-self and Source-expression concrete on the output/action side,
  which the current atlas mostly handles through perception, behavior tests,
  and service language.
- It is distinctive Shinzen material that a generic mindfulness atlas would
  miss.

Recommended public move:

- Do not make it the next public spine page before Total Happiness aim
  structure.
- Add a compact public page after the aim-structure pass if the atlas is
  allowed one specialized practice expansion.
- Link from `practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md`,
  `practice-method-safety.md`, `way-of-flow.md`, `source-service-and-
  bodhicitta.md`, and `glossary.md`.

Suggested public title:

- `Auto Output Practice`
- Alternate: `Auto Move, Auto Speak, and Auto Think`

### 3. Teaching As Service Ladder

Internal owner: [[Teaching A Path]]

Current atlas coverage:

- `shinzens-teaching-method.md` explains teaching style.
- `guidance-scope-and-accountability.md` explains role and safety boundaries.
- `mastery-without-guru-inflation.md` blocks status inflation.

Missing public delta:

- Teaching as a Total Happiness service branch has levels:
  subtle presence, coherent description, explicit instruction, professional
  teaching, enlightenment-edge competence, path discovery, and supporting
  teachers.
- Teacher selection is competence plus relationship, not map agreement alone.
- Track record matters, but track record still needs accountability.

Why it matters:

- The atlas already says service and teaching matter, but it does not yet show
  how Shinzen differentiates teaching roles.
- This would improve public judgment around "should I teach?", "is this person
  qualified?", and "does practice naturally teach through presence?"

Recommended public move:

- Add a section to `shinzens-teaching-method.md` first.
- Create `teaching-as-service.md` only if the section becomes too large.
- Link from `total-happiness.md`, `guidance-scope-and-accountability.md`,
  `mastery-without-guru-inflation.md`, and `source-service-and-bodhicitta.md`.

### 4. Discrimination And Unification / Empowering Contrasts

Internal owner: [[Discrimination and Unification]]

Current atlas coverage:

- Pieces appear across `sensory-clarity.md`, `complete-experience.md`,
  `flow-and-gone.md`, `expansion-and-contraction.md`,
  `deconstruction-and-reconstruction.md`, and `no-self-without-erasing-
  personality.md`.

Missing public delta:

- The explicit dialectic is under-carried: distinguish components, let CCE
  digest and unify them, then remain free to discriminate again.
- This answers a recurring advanced confusion: "is analysis the opposite of
  unity?"

Why it matters:

- It improves the public explanation of why Shinzen's technical vocabulary is
  not anti-nondual and why unity is not anti-discrimination.
- It is lower urgency than Total Happiness because the atlas already covers
  many of its child mechanisms.

Recommended public move:

- Add a compact "discrimination and unification" section to
  `sensory-clarity.md` or `deconstruction-and-reconstruction.md`.
- Promote to a page only if advanced reader-route tests show confusion.

### 5. Applied-Life Boundary Pack

Internal anchors:

- health/illness and cancer sources;
- birth and parenting sources;
- sleep interruption;
- sexuality and pleasure practice;
- lucid dreaming;
- caffeine and alertness;
- retreat aftershock;
- public news and world-facing emotion.

Current atlas coverage:

- `safety-scope-and-accountability.md`, `intensity-and-embodiment-safety.md`,
  `practice-method-safety.md`, and `practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md`
  name many of these domains.

Missing public delta:

- The atlas has the safety categories but not a public "applied life domains"
  map.

Why it matters:

- These domains are high-risk and easy to misuse.
- The compiler has more concrete boundary logic than the public atlas exposes.

Recommended public move:

- Do not expand this before the aim-structure pass unless a reader route
  demands it.
- If promoted, make it a boundary index, not advice pages: illness, sleep,
  sexuality, birth/parenting, substances/caffeine, lucid dreaming, public
  suffering, and retreat aftercare should route to ordinary support first.

Suggested public title:

- `Applied Life Boundaries`
