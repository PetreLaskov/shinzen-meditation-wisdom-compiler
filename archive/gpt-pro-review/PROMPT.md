# Prompt For GPT-5.5 Pro

I am giving you a context bundle from a Shinzen Young meditation-wisdom compiler
wiki.

Treat this as a review of an epistemic operating system for future agents. The
question is not "is this a nice wiki?" but "what prevents a future agent from
loading the fewest useful tokens and making the best calibrated
Shinzen-practice judgment?"

## Intent

Audit this wiki as an agent-maintained durable working-memory system, not as a
normal wiki, archive, biography project, transcript archive, generic Buddhism
encyclopedia, clinical guide, or comparative religion survey.

Primary optimization target:

Maximize future practice-reasoning quality, phenomenological discrimination,
transmission fidelity, safety calibration, source-grounded synthesis, and
review usefulness per token loaded.

## Review For

- Whether the operating contract is coherent, enforceable, and not overburdened.
- Whether `wiki/index.md` routes a future agent to the right few pages quickly.
- Whether `wiki/Current Model.md` is useful compression or too bloated to serve.
- Whether page types, frontmatter, links, aliases, `load_when`, and
  `best_linked_pages` support routing.
- Whether source pages preserve teaching moves, practice handles, weak claims,
  omissions, tensions, source frames, and model deltas.
- Whether synthesis pages overclaim, smooth contradictions, cite too diffusely,
  or rely on frontmatter as a hidden map.
- Whether safety, service, teacher-boundary, clinical-differential, and behavior
  verification questions are visible enough.
- Whether lint diagnostics reveal deeper structural debt.
- What should be split, merged, renamed, promoted, demoted, sub-indexed, or made
  into a better synthesis/question page.
- What small workflow or lint changes would give the biggest long-term gain.

## Do Not

- Turn this into generic Buddhism, Shinzen biography, or meditation commentary.
- Judge Shinzen's metaphysical claims as true or false unless the wiki itself is
  overclaiming them.
- Optimize for page count, source coverage for its own sake, or prettiness.
- Rewrite the whole system from scratch.
- Erase uncertainty, contradictions, source frames, safety boundaries, or
  source-use tiers.
- Treat raw sources as available unless they are included in this bundle.

## Deliver

1. Top 10 audit findings, ordered by impact.
2. Specific optimization recommendations.
3. Suggested file/page changes, grouped as quick wins, medium refactors, and
   major refactors.
4. Missing review criteria or lint checks to add to `AGENTS.md`,
   `wiki/_operations.md`, `wiki/_templates.md`, or `tools/wiki_lint.py`.
5. Pages that most deserve splitting, compression, sub-indexing, or stronger
   source posture.
6. A concise next-best review pass plan.

## Important Context

The wiki currently reports 212 compiled pages and 225 checked raw sources. Lint
passes mechanically, but diagnostics include a 77-source canonical backlog,
oversized `sources` frontmatter on several owner pages, and large domains that
may need syntheses or sub-indexing. See `lint-output-2026-05-12.txt`.

For raw-to-compiled fidelity, only audit the four source-page/raw-source pairs
included in this bundle. For the rest, evaluate structure, routing, source
posture, and synthesis quality from the compiled pages.
