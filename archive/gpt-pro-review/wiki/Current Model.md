This is the wiki's current whole-system synthesis after the compiled Basic Mindfulness manual, *The Science of Enlightenment* chapter arc, and See Hear Feel introduction. Its Shinzen claims depend on the compiled source pages and owner pages named below; staged transcripts and uncompiled raw files may later revise it, but they do not ground this page.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Shinzen's system is a sensory-completion model with two major interfaces: the older Basic Mindfulness grid and the newer See Hear Feel compression both train CCE, route sensory experience, support complete experience over time, and culminate in enlightenment language that must remain operational and map-humble.
- **Why this matters**: Future reasoning can route by source tier, practice interface, skill, focus range, technique, teaching move, life context, risk, and service outcome instead of reloading every source.
- **Key tensions**: SHF simplifies without eliminating hidden grid complexity; safety, dosage, clinical boundaries, coach competence, behavior verification, power ethics, Source overclaim, and future-science or mathematics claims remain under-specified.
- **Source posture**: Strong as Shinzen's own practice, teaching, and transformation architecture; probable as the wiki's synthesis of his system; speculative when used as metaphysics, empirical science, clinical guidance, power ontology, or universal comparative religion.

## Through-Line
The system begins with a pragmatic attention claim. Meditation strengthens baseline concentration, but Shinzen's mindfulness requires two further skills: sensory clarity and equanimity ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 2 - The Most Fundamental Skill]] S3). The manual makes the same triad its root definition of mindful awareness, then adds a practice success test: practice should reduce suffering, increase fulfillment, deepen self-knowledge, change behavior, or cultivate love and service in a reasonable time frame ([[Basic Mindfulness Introduction]] S2-S5).

The Sensory Grid turns that skill claim into a routing grammar. Experience can be discussed as mental image, mental talk, emotional body sensation, physical sight, physical sound, physical body sensation, rest, flow, and vanishing; in practice, labels point attention rather than define ultimate reality ([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 7 - A Science of Sensory Experience]] S3-S8; [[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 8 - Five More Ways]] S5). This makes "be mindful" actionable: choose a cell, column, row, all experience, SHF focus range, Gone, Do Nothing, or Nurture Positive.

The completed SHF introduction shifts the current interface without replacing the older model. The later system makes Focus on See, Hear, Feel, and Everything the standard beginner entry; interprets labels by focus range; allows exclusive or inclusive emphasis; normalizes missed, guessed, late, averaged, and repeated notes; and treats label strength, re-noting, zooming, stance, and explicit Gone as adjustable supports ([[See Hear Feel Introduction - Starting Focus Ranges]] S1-S5; [[See Hear Feel Introduction - Simple and Flexible Labels]] S4-S8; [[See Hear Feel Introduction - Four Okays and Required vs Allowed]] S1-S11; [[See Hear Feel Introduction - Noting Nutshell and FAQ]] S3-S8). Unit 08 gives the compatibility rule: the old grid remains valid and upwardly compatible, while the newer Unified Mindfulness interface hides and extends it through flexible labeling ([[See Hear Feel Introduction - Practice Organization and System Transition]] S7-S10).

The transformation claim is CCE over time. Chapter 5 states the local theorem: concentration plus sensory clarity plus equanimity plus time yields insight plus purification ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 5 - Insight and Purification]] S10). The mechanism is not that meditation creates special content, but that ordinary sensory events become complete enough to lose compulsive binding. Thought, fear, pleasure, pain, self, and behavior are all routed through trackable components, and "trackable implies tractable" is the operating logic ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 5 - Insight and Purification]] S3-S8).

Gate 3 turns that theorem into a sharper diagnostic. Complete experience is not inferred from extraordinary content, long sits, pain level, spontaneous movement, retreat aftershock, or bhanga. The better signs are critical-mass CCE from beginning to end, sensory circuits learning not to fight themselves, suffering dropping while sensory detail remains available, positive behavior or perception changing over time, and appropriate support when intensity exceeds the current practice frame ([[Zero and One - Part 1 of 4 Complete Experiences]] S5; [[Zero and One - Part 3 Strong Determination Challenges, Benefits, and Tasting Purification]] S3-S7; [[The Trickle-Down Paradigm of Transformation]] S6-S7; [[Kriyas & Complete Experiences]] S3-S9; [[Experiences of the Dissolution (Bhanga) Process - 2 of 3]] S2-S4).

Impermanence is the first deep reading of that completion process. Solid events can reveal movement, vibration, force, and Flow when met with concentration, clarity, and equanimity; vanishings become a dedicated object through Gone ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 6 - The Many Faces of Impermanence]] S8-S17; [[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 8 - Five More Ways]] S5-S6). Flow and Gone therefore complement each other: Flow emphasizes ongoing movement and force, while Gone emphasizes abrupt diminishing or passing ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 9 - The Power of Gone]] S5-S7).

Gate 4 now sharpens that layer. The deep impermanence route should be read as ordinary change becoming Flow, abrupt diminishing becoming Gone, pressure and apparent solidity becoming simultaneous Expansion-Contraction, and Source remaining an afterglow boundary rather than a direct sensory object. [[Surface To Source]] gives the compact route, while [[Impermanence Flow Gone And Source]] carries the post-Gate-4 synthesis.

No-self is the self-side version of the same model. Shinzen does not primarily deny that self-experience appears; he denies that it is a thing. Finite I-am-ness is modeled as mental image, mental talk, and emotional body sensation that can tangle into self-as-particle or clarify into fluid personality activity ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 8 - The Real No Self]] S1-S4, S7, S10-S11). The mature correction is not to hate self-arising but to clarify and allow it; self-arising and self-cessation both become practice material ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 8 - The Real No Self]] S14-S18).

Source is the system's deepest interpretive frame, not its safest evidence tier. Chapter 10 makes Source/Zero practice-legible through Gone and simultaneous Expansion-Contraction: sensory events arise through polar forces and return through cancellation, but Source itself is not experienced as an object because no self is present at the cancellation; what appears afterward is conditioned afterglow or after-representation ([[Science of Enlightenment Chapter 10 - Return to the Source]] S4, S12-S15). This boundary should govern looser Source language elsewhere.

Gate 6B starts by naming a non-duality routing rule. [[Non-Dual Awareness]]
says the phrase can mean a sense object without Feel/Image/Talk observer
reaction, a deeper formless merging where subject and object disappear into
Zero, or Shinzen's preferred mature use: ordinary life repeatedly cycling
from Zero into self/world polarization and back without a fundamental split
between Source and daily activity ([[Non-Dual Awareness]] S1-S5). Future
path-map and witness sources should preserve that ambiguity instead of
treating non-dual awareness as one generic state.

Gate 6B item 2 adds the self-inquiry bridge. [[Self-Enquiry and Mindfulness
Meditation]] says self-enquiry turns awareness back toward the origin of an
arising, while mindfulness can approach the same Source-facing boundary by
noticing Gone: where A vanishes is where B arises. This keeps self-inquiry
inside the Shinzen model without making it mandatory, since the talk says it
works well for some people but not everyone ([[Self-Enquiry and Mindfulness
Meditation]] S1-S6).

Gate 6B item 3 adds the witness router. [[The Absolute Witness]] says the
detached witness is a useful distance-creating equanimity metaphor, but it is
still produced by subtle Feel/Image/Talk. The absolute witness is contentless
cessation, Zero, nirodha, Source, or emptiness; the practice bridge is to
detect the disappearance of the subtle Feel/Image/Talk that creates an
observer ([[The Absolute Witness]] S2-S7).

Gate 6B item 4 adds the selflessness progression. [[A Deeper Freedom
Experiences of Selflessness]] stages no-self as Feel/Image/Talk
disentangling, then wave-like de-coagulation, then stopping into Zero, then
the deeper freedom of Feel/Image/Talk re-arising from Zero as unblocked
healthy personality rather than a coagulated ego-problem ([[A Deeper Freedom
Experiences of Selflessness]] S1-S5).

Gate 6B item 5 adds the humility-to-vanishing comparative frame. [[Humility
to the Vanishing Point No Self Around the World]] reads Sufi, Jewish,
Christian, Buddhist, East Asian, and Taoist no-self or emptiness terms as
parallel ways of naming the vanishing of self/world somethingness, while
preserving the return side: *baqa*, continuous arising from divine nothing,
*theosis* as goodness and compassion, and "nano-Christ" service
([[Humility to the Vanishing Point No Self Around the World]] S1-S5). This
does not prove cross-tradition identity; it strengthens Shinzen's translation
strategy and service-facing no-self model.

Gate 6B item 6 adds the no-place-to-stand practice condition. [[No Place to
Stand]] says Shinzen's refusal to give a breath-like home base is sometimes
intentional: equanimity metaphors and anchors can help, but the fixed
riverbank watcher can become a position. When some concentration, clarity,
and equanimity are present, not finding a standpoint may support decentering,
figure-ground reversal, and "the center" having the practitioner rather than
the practitioner securing a center ([[No Place to Stand]] S1-S7). Without
that CCE condition, groundlessness remains a method-fit and safety question.

Gate 6B item 7 adds the ox-herding path-map router. [[Enlightenment and the
Ten Zen Ox Herding Pictures]] maps ordinary searching as condition-dependent
happiness seeking, footprints as indications of another possibility,
partial ox-sighting as glimpse, catching the ox as first no-self, stream
entry, or kensho, taming as stabilization, riding the ox backwards as
surrender to impermanence without needing orientation, homecoming as repose,
and the final three pictures as no substance, ordinary appearance, and
marketplace service ([[Enlightenment and the Ten Zen Ox Herding Pictures]]
S1-S5). This source strengthens the rule that path maps should end in
ordinary functioning and service, not in private attainment status.

Gate 6B item 8 confirms the opening half of that map from the first
three-part ox-herding source. [[Zen Ox-Herding Pics - Part 1 of 3]] is
narrower and apparently truncated, but it makes the front-end diagnostic
especially clear: ordinary search misidentifies condition-independent
happiness as condition-dependent objects; footprints, partial sighting, and
catching the ox should not be collapsed into one attainment category ([[Zen
Ox-Herding Pics - Part 1 of 3]] S1-S5).

Gate 6B item 9 adds the middle and late-stage ox-herding diagnostic from
[[Zen Ox-Herding Pics - Part 2 of 3]]. Taming means enlightenment has been
internalized enough that it does not slip away, but riding the ox backward is
the stronger surrender move: letting impermanence carry experience without
needing orientation or answers. The same source gives the homecoming/repose
stage and begins the final triad with no substance and ordinary appearance,
while leaving the final use/service picture for part 3 ([[Zen Ox-Herding
Pics - Part 2 of 3]] S1-S5).

Gate 6B item 10 completes the ox-herding checkpoint. [[Zen Ox-Herding Pics -
Part 3 of 3]] makes entering the marketplace with hand outstretched the final
cause of practice: ordinary approachable presence, the effects of practice
communicated through one's life, coherent description when asked, and
eventual teaching for some people when students appear. [[Ten Ox-Herding
Pictures]] now carries the reusable map, while [[Operational Enlightenment]]
and [[Guidance Scope and Accountability Boundary]] keep service and teaching
claims bounded by consent, behavior, feedback, role clarity, and
anti-dependency ([[Zen Ox-Herding Pics - Part 3 of 3]] S1-S5).

Gate 6A adds an enlightenment posture rather than a final attainment map. [[What Is Enlightenment]] defines liberation as identity no longer being trapped by thoughts and body sensations while body-mind remains a home, [[Enlightenment Maps and Models]] says every existing map is still poor and provisional, [[After Enlightenment What Is Left What Is The Point]] says sudden no-self still leaves a lifetime of behavior refinement and daily-life integration, [[Enlightenment Downsides]] says enlightenment is neither continuous bliss/perfection nor an influence one can personally own, [[Six Common Traps on the Path to Enlightenment]] says maps, fundamentalist certainty, tranquility, powers, enlightenment, and observer identity can all become good-place traps, [[Classical Enlightenment Healing the World and Screw-ups]] says mass stream entry might heal the world over long time while individual no-self still permits public screw-ups, [[Enlightenment DPDR and Falling Into the Pit of the Void]] says no-self and paper-thin world can resemble DPDR while having the opposite effect, [[Classic Dark Night or Clinical Issues]] says apparent dark-night distress usually may include ordinary depression, anxiety, or clinical factors requiring competent assessment, [[Advanced FAQs Regarding Emptiness]] says advanced pit-of-emptiness distress should be met by emptying the reaction and intentionally reconstructing positive Feel/Image/Talk, [[Meditation Teacher's Qualifications and Liberation Experiences]] says liberation signs do not prove teacher qualification because behavior and student results matter more than body language, [[Spiritual Teachers' Behaviour Feedback and Ethics]] says teacher ethics depends on open behavioral feedback channels because uneven development persists when correction is blocked or refused, and [[Teacher's or Student's Issues Impeding Spiritual Progress]] says teacher dependence blocks the path because competent liberation teaching should produce independent peers. The current model should therefore use enlightenment language operationally, ask what a map makes possible, and avoid converting Shinzen's definitions into final map authority, personal completion, immediate world repair, spiritual status, clinical certainty, teacher qualification, ethics self-certification, teacher dependence, or emptiness-only human-function erosion.

The final model returns to ordinary life. Total happiness includes ordinary
surface well-being and deep happiness for oneself and others; the dedicated
Total Happiness close now defines teaching as fostering others'
extraordinary happiness through subtle presence, coherent description,
explicit instruction, competence around awakening edges, rare path discovery,
and supporting teachers. Guidance includes rapport, strategy selection,
behavior commitments, focus methods, method-choice principles, and follow-up
([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 11 - Life]] S7-S9; [[Total Happiness - 5 of 5 -
The Activity of Teaching]] S1-S5; [[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 12 - The Big
Picture]] S10-S13; [[See Hear Feel Introduction - Practice Organization and
System Transition]] S4-S6). If insight, Source, no-self, Flow, or SHF fluency
do not improve behavior, responsiveness, support, teaching competence, or
service, that is a model warning rather than a secondary detail.

## Practice Routing
Future practice reasoning should usually route in this order:

- **Source tier**: Is the task asking for Shinzen's instruction, the wiki's synthesis, a clinical/scientific claim, a metaphysical claim, or a comparative-tradition claim?
- **Interface**: Is the better entry the older Basic Mindfulness grid, the newer SHF focus-range interface, Do Nothing, Nurture Positive, Gone, or a guidance/life-practice frame?
- **Skill**: Is the live need concentration, sensory clarity, equanimity, or balance among them?
- **Object scope**: Is the useful focus a grid cell, column, row, all experience, SHF range, specialized range, Space, Flow, Gone, Do Nothing, or Nurture Positive?
- **Technique support**: Are labels helping CCE, and should they be dropped, strengthened, spoken, repeated, widened, narrowed, made exclusive/inclusive, zoomed, or contextualized by stance?
- **Effort release**: Is the practitioner caught in option choice, racy Noting, or voluntary attention-control, and would Do Nothing help; or is Do Nothing becoming spacey, agitating, or clinically concerning enough to switch, stop, or add support?
- **Strategy**: Should guidance turn toward the challenge through deconstruction, Flow, or reconstruction, or turn away through grounding, Rest, positive Feel, Do Nothing, external anchors, or Gone-related relief; and does [[Turn Toward and Turn Away]] show enough permission, clarity, equanimity, and ordinary support for the selected branch ([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 12 - The Big Picture]] S12)?
- **Transmission context**: Is the question generic, or does it depend on the student's current technique, focus range, capacity, Wall/Window, and life situation?
- **Risk**: Is the practice strengthening clarity, embodiment, response, learning, feedback, and support, or drifting into suppression, dissociation, macho endurance, intensity-chasing, Flow fixation, Gone distress, positivity bypass, medical neglect, teacher overreach, closed feedback, or Source inflation?
- **Void/dark-night differential**: Are no-self and emptiness empowering, meaningful, functional, and reconstructively integrated, or DPDR-like, nihilistic, derealizing, depersonalizing, disabling, depressed, anxious, clinically mixed, motivation-eroding, or too intense to process unaided?
- **Life test**: What behavior commitment, relationship repair, service action, support check, or ordinary practical step would show that practice is integrating?

This routing keeps Shinzen's system modular without turning it into a menu of disconnected techniques.

## Confidence Tiers
- **Established as Shinzen's stated system**: CCE as mindful awareness; the older Sensory Grid; the newer SHF focus-range and flexible-label interface; Noting, Do Nothing, and Nurture Positive; the Five Ways; Gone as noticed vanishing; Four Okays; and the total-happiness/guidance architecture are directly supported by compiled primary source pages.
- **Probable as the wiki's current synthesis**: The best whole-system model is sensory completion with interface evolution: CCE plus sensory routing leads to complete experience, which over time supports insight, purification, impermanence, no-self, behavior change, and service.
- **Source-attributed and safety-sensitive**: Flow as purifier/guide/unifier, Gone as indirect Source contact, no-self as fluid personality, Space as perfection/Stillpoint, operational enlightenment as identity non-capture, altered phenomena as upaya, bhanga, primordial Feel, free-shifting during high activation, retreat equivalence, coach competence, and spontaneous service are important inside Shinzen's frame but require careful boundaries.
- **Speculative or contested outside the frame**: Source/Zero as objective metaphysics, powers/entities/past lives, science-spirituality parallels, future enlightenment technology, broad neuroscience claims, mathematics-based inner-experience modeling, universal comparative mysticism, and clinical or developmental interpretations are not established by the compiled sources. Gate 6A item 2 strengthens Shinzen's own caution that current enlightenment maps are not final.

## Tensions And Frontiers
- [[See Hear Feel]] - SHF is now the current compact interface, but its simplicity depends on hidden focus-range structure; ambiguity returns when the active range, label mode, or old/new terminology is unclear.
- [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] - the central open question: how to distinguish complete experience from suppression, dissociation, indifference, medical neglect, self-erasure, energy fixation, unsupported dissolution, Source overclaim, or positivity bypass.
- [[Practice Guidance Toolkit]] - guidance is one of the system's strongest applied layers, but scope of practice, consent, trauma routing, medical referral, coach or teacher competence, and behavior verification are not operationalized.
- [[Intermediate Realm]] - altered power phenomena are routed as sensory practice material, not proof or status, but clinical differentials and power-service ethics remain open.
- [[Source And Polarities]] - the polarity model is central and useful, yet chapter 10's afterglow boundary and explicit science caution prevent direct-object or objective-reality overclaim.
- [[Operational Enlightenment]] - early Gate 6A evidence now combines identity non-capture, map humility, post-realization integration, anti-projection/creditless-influence boundaries, good-place trap diagnosis, stream-entry-scale world-healing calibration, a DPDR/pit-of-the-void differential, dark-night clinical-referral posture, emptiness-reconstruction regimen, teacher-qualification boundary, feedback-loop ethics, and anti-dependency, while formal teacher-conduct standards remain underdeveloped.
- [[Total Happiness]] and [[Teaching A Path]] - service is structural, but the sources do not define how to verify that insight, teaching, claimed power, or Source-based care actually improves conduct and avoids harm.

## What Would Change The Model
- Stronger source evidence for Shinzen's own diagnostic criteria around dosage, stopping, grounding, referral, trauma, dissociation, medical pain, mania/psychosis risk, altered phenomena, bhanga, retreats, accelerators, and teacher/coach competence would sharpen the safety model; Gate 3 makes these criteria more urgent but does not supply them.
- Current clinical evidence and Shinzen-specific case criteria for DPDR, pit-of-the-void distress, dark night, depression, anxiety, medication/substance effects, and meditation-linked derealization would sharpen the new Gate 6A differential.
- Behavioral or practice-case evidence showing reliable links between CCE, complete experience, purification, behavior change, and service would raise confidence in the transformation claims.
- Evidence that Source, powers, future neuroscience, mathematics-based inner-experience models, or cross-tradition parallels are more than Shinzen's interpretive frame would raise those tiers; counterexamples or harms from those frames would lower them.
- Later Shinzen lecture ingests may revise live routing details for Gone, Flow, expansion-contraction, total happiness, service, dark-night support, teacher ethics, and teaching register. They should preserve practice handles and update owner pages before this synthesis is revised again.
