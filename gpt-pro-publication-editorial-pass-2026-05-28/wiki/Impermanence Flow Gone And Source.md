Impermanence Flow Gone And Source is the first-load index for Shinzen's change
cluster. It routes a report or question to the right owner page before future
agents load [[Impermanence]], [[Flow]], [[Gone]], [[Spaciousness]],
[[Expansion And Contraction]], [[Dissolution]], or Source boundary pages.

## Key Points
- **Core claim**: Impermanence is a practice route: ordinary changingness can
  become Flow, abrupt drop-off can become Gone, openness or thinness can
  route through Space, pressure can refine into Expansion-Contraction, and
  Source belongs under afterglow and service boundaries.
- **Why this matters**: The cluster is easy to over-load. A practitioner
  report about "energy," "space," "gone," "dissolution," or "Source" should
  descend only to the child page that changes the decision.
- **Key tensions**: Flow can become energy chasing, Gone can become void
  fixation, Space can be confused with dissociation or formless attainment,
  Expansion-Contraction can become cosmology, and bhanga can be overread as
  status instead of risk-sensitive practice material.
- **Source posture**: Probable as a synthesis of compiled owner pages and Gate
  4 sources; speculative when extended to science, metaphysics, clinical
  diagnosis, or verified service outcomes.

## Route Sequence

The safe explanatory order is ordinary sensory change first. An itch, breath,
pulse, sound, image, talk burst, body sensation, emotional Feel, or field of
Rest can be known as changing before it becomes advanced vocabulary. Shinzen's
chapter 6 frame says impermanence becomes liberating when concentration,
sensory clarity, equanimity, and time make solidity reveal movement, process,
purification, unification, and Source-wave freedom ([[Science of
Enlightenment Chapter 6 - The Many Faces of Impermanence]] S3-S19).

When change presents as ongoing movement, energy, vibration, or force, route
to [[Flow]]. Flow is the positive or activated side of impermanence, and it
can appear in ordinary sensory zones rather than only in dramatic energy
states ([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 4 - The Way of Flow]] S1-S10; [[The
Focus on Flow Theme]] S1-S4).

When change presents as abrupt ending or diminishing, route to [[Gone]]. Gone
does not require grand cessation. Breath endings, mental-talk endings, partial
drops, and non-permanent abrupt diminishings are sufficient practice objects
when the passing is detected ([[Basic Mindfulness Chapter 8 - Five More
Ways]] S5-S9; [[Abrupt Flow Diminishings, Vanishings and Noting Gone]]
S1-S6).

When the salient feature is openness around or thinness within experience,
route to [[Spaciousness]] before forcing Flow or Source language. Shinzen made
Space independent in the later SHF grid because spaciousness can be stable,
dynamic, or polarity-like, and classical formless absorption language does
not necessarily include Flow or impermanence ([[See Hear Feel Introduction -
Five Themes Space and Depth Boundary]] S1-S5; [[How Shinzen Uses the Term
Spaciousness]] S1-S5).

When change presents as spreading, gripping, squeezing, scattering, or both
directions at once, route to [[Expansion And Contraction]]. This is both a
practical Flow taxonomy and the bridge into the deeper polarity frame. It is
especially relevant when flat arising-and-passing leaves an observer watching
from outside, because the spherical force frame includes observer and world
inside the same process ([[Born Between Expansion and Contraction]] S1-S3;
[[Paradigms of Change]] S5-S6; [[Mindfulness Momentum, Arising and Passing
to Simultaneous Expansion and Contraction]] S5-S7).

[[Shinzen Young Retreat at the Monastic Academy 05.27.2017]] gives the
retreat-stream practice route into that bridge. After ordinary See/Hear/Feel
and Note Everything warmup, Shinzen has practitioners track arisings,
passings, simultaneous arising-passing, and rest as yes/no/both/rest in one
modality or across all modalities. Continuous arisings may become effortless
spreading, continuous passings effortless collapsing, and both/rest a
Source-facing polarity cycle ([[Shinzen Young Retreat at the Monastic
Academy 05.27.2017]] S3-S10). This belongs after ordinary impermanence
practice, not before safety and fit checks.

[[When Sensory Experience Loses Its Something-ness]] gives the selected
source-page anchor for A&P questions in ordinary language. Shinzen frames
rising-passing as a stage or availability where inner and outer events lose
their opaque somethingness into transparent doingness, with emptiness,
essencelessness, and effortlessness as experiential gates rather than mere
doctrinal labels ([[When Sensory Experience Loses Its Something-ness]]
S3-S6). The same source keeps householder pacing visible before any severe
ego-death interpretation (S7).

When ordinary sensory order loosens into blissful vibration, harsh chaos,
loss of center, fear, or post-retreat aftershock, route to [[Dissolution]]
and safety pages. Bhanga may be absent, heavenly, purgatorial, or mixed; it
needs support and differential caution rather than status comparison
([[Dissolution (Bhanga), and T.S. Eliot]] S2-S5; [[Experiences of the
Dissolution (Bhanga) Process - 2 of 3]] S1-S4).

When Source language appears, descend by claim type. If the issue is whether
Source or Zero is directly experienced, use [[Source Afterglow Boundary]]. If
the issue is science, mathematics, nature, or time-space analogy, use [[Source
Science And Analogy Boundary]]. If the issue is service, larger identity,
love, or action justified by Source, use [[Source And Service Boundary]].

## Sibling-Load Rules

Do not load every sibling page just because one impermanence word appears:

- Load [[Flow]] when the decision depends on ongoing movement, energy, force,
  purification taste, or Flow chasing.
- Load [[Gone]] when the decision depends on endings, vanishings,
  note-til-Gone, cessation, Source-facing passing, or void reactions.
- Load [[Spaciousness]] when the decision depends on openness, thinness,
  Space, formless absorption translation, or Space-versus-Flow boundaries.
- Load [[Expansion And Contraction]] when the decision depends on force
  direction, Both, observer-including polarity, or pressure/gripping.
- Load [[Dissolution]] when the report is bhanga-like, aftershock-like,
  blissfully vibratory, harshly chaotic, or status-comparison prone.
- Load [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] before any deep-map
  interpretation when distress, dissociation, medical/clinical risk, teacher
  pressure, practice worsening, behavior mismatch, or ordinary functioning
  loss is present.

## Tensions And Frontiers

- [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] - Flow, Gone, no-self, bhanga,
  Source afterglow, and surrender language still need clarity, embodiment,
  behavior, support, and clinical-differential checks.
- [[Source Afterglow Boundary]] - chapter 10's afterglow rule should govern
  Source-as-object claims even when earlier or oral sources sound more direct.
- [[Source Science And Analogy Boundary]] - Shinzen's mathematics,
  neuroscience, cosmology, shunya/kenosis, and Pythagorean language remains
  analogy or aspiration rather than established proof.
- [[Source And Service Boundary]] - service and improving self/world are
  maturity tests, but the compiled sources still require behavior,
  accountability, consent, and competence checks.
