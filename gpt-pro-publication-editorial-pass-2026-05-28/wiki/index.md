# Index

This wiki tracks Shinzen Young's meditation practice and wisdom system as a
compiled working model, not as a transcript archive, biography file, clinical
guide, or general Buddhism encyclopedia. Route first by practice handle,
phenomenological distinction, technique choice, teaching move, transformation
mechanism, safety boundary, source tier, and service implication.

The current whole-system through-line is [[Current Model]]: Shinzen's system
is best modeled as sensory completion with interface evolution. CCE trains
attention, the older Sensory Grid and newer See Hear Feel route experience,
complete experience plus time drives insight and purification, impermanence
and self models route through Flow, Gone, no-self, Source, and service, and
operational enlightenment remains map-humble, behavior-accountable,
anti-dependent, and clinically bounded.

Live guidance routing: for a concrete practitioner report, load [[Practice
Guidance Toolkit]] before giving advice. If the report includes medical or
clinical risk, severe dissociation or void distress, self-harm or harm risk,
coercive teacher pressure, practice worsening, or insight without behavior
improvement, load [[Complete Experience Safety Boundary]] first; for teacher,
guidance, consent, or accountability concerns, load [[Guidance Scope and
Accountability Boundary]] before optimizing technique.

For exhaustive compiled-page registration, source-page lookup, and broad
domain scans, use `wiki/_page_catalog.md`. The index is the first-load router;
the catalog is the archive/register surface.
