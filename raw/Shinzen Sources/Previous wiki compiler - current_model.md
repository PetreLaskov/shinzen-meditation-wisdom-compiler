# Current Model

This is the startup state vector, not the encyclopedia. Load owner pages when a task needs mechanism, evidence, examples, or safety nuance.

## Active Beliefs

- Mindfulness in Shinzen's broad modern frame is best treated as trait-level concentration, sensory clarity, and equanimity working together, not mere calm, presence branding, or a one-term translation of one Asian word.
- A mindfulness system needs theory, techniques, and application guidelines; Basic Mindfulness supplies that through the Sensory Grid, Noting, Do Nothing, Nurture Positive, and life-practice routing.
- The Sensory Grid is pragmatic classification: See/Hear/Feel crossed with In/Out/Rest/Flow, plus Gone as the noticed transition of vanishing rather than continued absence.
- Noting is noticing plus intentional knowing; labeling is adjustable support, not the essence of the technique.
- Do Nothing regulates effort by dropping controllable intentions to direct attention; Nurture Positive reconstructs wholesome image, talk, and feeling patterns so deconstruction is not the whole path.
- Complete experience is the central transformation hinge: sensory events met with concentration, clarity, equanimity, and time are claimed to reduce suffering, untangle self/world solidity, and support insight plus purification.
- Flow and Gone are complementary impermanence routes. Flow reveals movement, force, energy, and dynamic change; Gone reveals abrupt diminishment and cessation.
- No-self means no thing-self, not no personality. The finite self is inner activity, especially image, talk, and body emotion, that can congeal into a self-particle or clarify into fluid personality.
- Source/Zero and expansion-contraction are powerful practice models but speculative as metaphysics, physics, biology, or mathematics. Zero is not directly experienced; wisdom returns as conditioned after-representation.
- The intermediate realm of blockage and power is practice material, not proof or status. Visions, energies, entities, bliss, and psychic-seeming phenomena are decomposed into sensory events, Flow, and Gone.
- Total happiness links private sensory training to behavior and service: surface and deep happiness for oneself and others remain the practical test domain.
- Practice selection is contextual. Turn-toward, turn-away, grounding, Flow, Gone, reconstruction, teacher support, therapy, recovery work, and community are all part of the support ecology.
- Shinzen's cross-cultural and future-science frame is useful as an integrative hypothesis, not established comparative religion, current neuroscience, medicine, or technology.
- The wiki treats raw transcripts as staged leads until compiled by one source page; staged raw files may guide search but do not ground durable claims.
- Context hygiene is now part of the operating model: route before reading, keep startup pages compact, and put details on owning pages.

## Active Tensions

- Complete experience is the strongest internal hinge, but the wiki still needs operational criteria that distinguish realization from suppression, dissociation, endurance, positivity bypass, energetic fixation, or self-confirming transformation stories.
- Safety remains under-specified around DPDR, dark night, bhanga, power phenomena, trauma activation, mania/psychosis differentials, medical issues, dosage, and when to stop or seek support.
- The CCE definition is clearer than before but not yet a rigorous measurement model.
- Cross-cultural, Source, polarity, and future-neuroscience claims need independent evidence before being treated as more than Shinzen's useful interpretive frame.
- Behavioral validation and ethical feedback remain crucial because insight, power, conduct, and service can develop unevenly.
